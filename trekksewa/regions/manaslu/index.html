Manaslu & Tsum मनास्लु

A full Himalayan circuit with far fewer people.

Manaslu and Tsum: flood damage in the Budhi Gandaki gorge

A flood on 11 October 2026 destroyed bridges and stretches of trail between Machha Khola and Philim and in lower Tsum. Temporary bypasses were reported on the Manaslu Circuit in the weeks that followed; access to Tsum was still uncertain when we last checked.

What to do: Ask your agency for the state of the gorge trail and the Tsum junction this week, and do not set out in or just after heavy rain.

Reported 15 November 2026. We have not re-checked it since.

The Manaslu circuit follows the Budhi Gandaki gorge from the mid-hills to the Tibetan-influenced villages of Nubri, crosses the Larkya La at 5,106 m and drops to the Annapurna side. The side valley of Tsum, opened to trekkers in 2008, keeps a strong Buddhist culture.

Both are restricted areas: you need a special permit and a licensed guide, arranged through a registered agency. Since March 2026 the permit can be issued to a single trekker. Lodges now cover every stage, but they are simpler than in Everest or Annapurna.

When to go

March to May and mid-September to November. The gorge section is landslide-prone in the monsoon, and the Larkya La is usually snowbound from late December to February.

Getting there

A long day by bus or jeep from Kathmandu to Soti Khola or Machha Khola. The trek ends on the Annapurna Circuit road at Dharapani.

Permits used in Manaslu

All permits

Manaslu RAP

Restricted area

Manaslu restricted area permit · Department of Immigration

September to NovemberUSD 100person for the first 7 days, then USD 15 a day
December to AugustUSD 75person for the first 7 days, then USD 10 a day

Where: Department of Immigration in Kathmandu (the Pokhara immigration office also issues them), through a registered trekking agency only.

Since 22 March 2026 the permit can be issued to a single trekker; before that the minimum was two. You still have to book through a registered agency and walk with a licensed guide, with at most seven trekkers per guide. The restricted area is Chumnubri Rural Municipality: it starts at Jagat and continues over the Larkya La. You need the MCAP and the ACAP as well.

Agency required

MCAP

Conservation area

Manaslu Conservation Area Permit · National Trust for Nature Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, single entry

Where: Online at NTNC's e-permit site or at the Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu. In practice your agency arranges it with the restricted-area permit.

The fee includes tax and children under 10 do not need a permit. The Manaslu circuit finishes inside the Annapurna Conservation Area, so you need an ACAP as well.

ACAP

Conservation area

Annapurna Conservation Area Permit · National Trust for Nature Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, single entry

Where: Online at NTNC's e-permit site, or in person at the Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, and the Nepal Tourism Board office in Pokhara. A permit issued at a trail checkpost costs double.

The fee includes tax and children under 10 do not need a permit. Paying online by card adds a 2.9% gateway charge. Covers every trek in the Annapurna range and Lower Mustang; Upper Mustang and Nar Phu need a restricted-area permit as well.

Tsum Valley RAP

Restricted area

Tsum Valley restricted area permit · Department of Immigration

September to NovemberUSD 40person for the first 7 days, then USD 7 a day
December to AugustUSD 30person for the first 7 days, then USD 7 a day

Where: Department of Immigration in Kathmandu (the Pokhara immigration office also issues them), through a registered trekking agency only.

Since 22 March 2026 the permit can be issued to a single trekker; before that the minimum was two. You still have to book through a registered agency and walk with a licensed guide, with at most seven trekkers per guide. Covers the Tsum wards of Chumnubri Rural Municipality (3, 6 and 7). If you carry on round the Manaslu circuit you need the Manaslu permit too.
