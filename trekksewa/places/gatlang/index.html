Gatlang गत्लाङ

A large Tamang village of closely packed stone and timber houses on a terraced hillside, the best-known stop on the Tamang Heritage Trail. Community lodges and homestays take guests, and the small sacred lake of Parvati Kunda lies a short climb above.
