Gorak Shep गोरक शेप

A handful of lodges on a frozen lakebed, the highest place to sleep on the trek and the base for both Everest Base Camp and Kala Patthar. Water is scarce and everything is expensive.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
