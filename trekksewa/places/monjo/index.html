Monjo मोन्जो

The last village before the Sagarmatha National Park gate, where park and municipality permits are checked. A quieter place to sleep than Phakding if you want a shorter climb to Namche.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
