ManasluVillage

Sama Gaun सामागाउँ

The main village of Nubri, a long street of stone houses below the Manaslu glacier and the standard place for a rest day. Side trips lead to Birendra Tal, Pungyen Gompa and Manaslu Base Camp.

Also written Samagaon, Samagaun, Sama, Ro

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
