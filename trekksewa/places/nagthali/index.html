Nagthali नागथली

A ridge-top meadow at 3,165 m and the high point of the Tamang Heritage Trail, with a small gompa and a few lodges. The view takes in Langtang Lirung, the Kerung peaks across the Tibetan border, Ganesh Himal and the Sanjen range.

Also written Nagthali Ghyang, Nagthali Danda
