Tarkeghyang तार्केघ्याङ

The largest Hyolmo village in Helambu, on a shelf below Ama Yangri with a monastery at its centre. Most of its houses were destroyed by the 2015 earthquake and have been rebuilt; a rough jeep road now reaches it from Timbu.

Also written Tarke Ghyang, Tarkegyang
