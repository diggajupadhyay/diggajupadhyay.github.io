Gokyo Fifth Lake

Ngozumpa Tsho, two and a half to three hours north of Gokyo. The moraine crest beside it, known as Scoundrel's Viewpoint, looks up the glacier to Cho Oyu and across to Everest, with more of the mountain showing than from Gokyo Ri.
