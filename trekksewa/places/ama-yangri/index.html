Ama Yangri आमा याङ्री

A 3,771 m summit above Tarkeghyang, revered by the Hyolmo as the protecting mother of Helambu. A stupa and prayer flags mark the top, which looks north to the Langtang, Dorje Lakpa and Jugal peaks.

Also written Aama Yangri, Yangri Peak
