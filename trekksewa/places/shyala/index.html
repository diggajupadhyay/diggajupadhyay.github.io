ManasluVillage

Shyala श्याला

A cluster of newer lodges on an open terrace ringed by peaks: Himalchuli and Ngadi Chuli to the south, Manaslu ahead and the Ganesh range behind. An alternative overnight stop to Lho.

Also written Syala
