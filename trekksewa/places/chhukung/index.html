Chhukung छुकुङ

The last lodges in the Imja valley, on a gravel flat between the moraines of the Lhotse and Nuptse glaciers. It is the base for Chhukung Ri, the Kongma La and Island Peak, and climbing gear for the peak can be hired here.

Also written Chukhung
