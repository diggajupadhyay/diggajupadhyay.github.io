Chandragiri चन्द्रागिरि

A 2,551 m hilltop on the south-west rim, reached by cable car from Thankot or by a steep forest trail of three to four hours. The top has a Shiva temple, viewing platforms and, on clear days, a skyline from the Annapurnas to Everest.
