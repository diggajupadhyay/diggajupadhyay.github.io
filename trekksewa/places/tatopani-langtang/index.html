LangtangVillage

Tatopani तातोपानी

A lodge village named for its hot springs, long the main draw of the Tamang Heritage Trail. The springs stopped flowing after the 2015 earthquake and reports of their return are unconfirmed, so ask in Gatlang before counting on a soak.

Also written Tatopani (Rasuwa), Tatopani (Tamang Heritage Trail)
