Renjo La रेन्जो ला

The westernmost of the three passes, between Gokyo and the Thame valley. A built stone staircase covers the steepest ground on the west side, and the view east from the top takes in Everest, Lhotse and Makalu above the Gokyo lake.

Also written Renjo La Pass
