LangtangVillage

Kyanjin Gompa क्यान्जिन गुम्बा

The last settlement in the Langtang valley: a wide cluster of lodges around a small monastery and a yak-cheese factory, ringed by Langtang Lirung, Kimshung and Naya Kanga. Every side trip in the upper valley starts here.

Also written Kyangjin Gompa, Kyanjin
