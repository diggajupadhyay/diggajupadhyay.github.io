Phoksundo Lake फोक्सुण्डो ताल

Nepal's deepest lake, about 145 m, filling a steep-sided valley at 3,612 m. Its colour shifts from turquoise to deep blue through the day, and apart from Ringmo its shore is empty.

Also written Shey Phoksundo Lake, Phoksundo Tal
