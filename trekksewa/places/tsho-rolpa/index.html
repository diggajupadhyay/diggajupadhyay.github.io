Tsho Rolpa च्छो रोल्पा

A grey-green glacial lake about three and a half kilometres long, held back by a moraine at the snout of the Trakarding Glacier. It is one of the largest glacial lakes in Nepal and among the most closely monitored.

Also written Tso Rolpa, Chho Rolpa
