Phulchoki फूलचोकी

The highest hill on the valley rim, at about 2,780 m, above Godavari on the southern side. A rough road and a steep forest trail both lead to the top, which is known for rhododendrons in March and April and for birdwatching.

Also written Phulchowki, Pulchoki
