Paiya

A lodge stop in forest on the hillside south of Lukla, between the Kari La shoulder and the Chutok La. In recent seasons the jeep track from the south has ended at Thamdanda, a short walk before Paiya; ask where it stops now.

Also written Puiyan, Paiyan
