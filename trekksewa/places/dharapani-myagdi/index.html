Dharapani धारापानी

A ridge-top village above the Myagdi Khola that gives the first clear look at Dhaulagiri and Gurja Himal. Not the Dharapani on the Annapurna Circuit; this one is a few hours' walk above Darbang.

Also written Dharapani (Myagdi)
