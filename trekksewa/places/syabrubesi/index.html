Syabrubesi स्याफ्रुबेँसी

The roadhead where the Langtang Khola meets the Bhote Koshi, and the classic start for Langtang and the Tamang Heritage Trail. The Bhote Koshi flood of 26 August 2026 badly damaged the town and cut its road; check access before you plan around it.

Also written Syaphrubesi, Syabru Besi
