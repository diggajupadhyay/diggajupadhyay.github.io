Annapurna Base Camp अन्नपूर्ण आधार शिविर

A cluster of lodges on the moraine of the South Annapurna Glacier, ringed by Annapurna I, Annapurna South, Hiunchuli and Machhapuchhre. At sunrise the light comes down the 8,091 m face directly above.

Also written ABC, Annapurna Sanctuary
