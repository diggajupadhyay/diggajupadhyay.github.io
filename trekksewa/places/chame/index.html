Chame चामे

The headquarters of Manang district, with shops, a bank, a health post and small hot springs across the river. Many trekkers now begin walking here after a jeep ride from Besisahar.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
