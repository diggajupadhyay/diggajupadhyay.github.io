RolwalingLodge settlement

Na ना गाउँ

The summer settlement of the Rolwaling Sherpas on a broad valley floor, lived in from about May to October. A few lodges open in the trekking seasons and serve as the base for Tsho Rolpa and the Yalung valley.

Also written Na Gaon, Nagaon
