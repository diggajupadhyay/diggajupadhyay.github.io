Cholangpati

A few lodges at the tree line on the ridge between Chandanbari and Laurebina, quoted at 3,584 m or about 3,650 m, where the forest gives way to open hillside. A sensible place to break the climb to Gosaikunda if Laurebina feels too high for the night.

Also written Cholang Pati, Chalang Pati
