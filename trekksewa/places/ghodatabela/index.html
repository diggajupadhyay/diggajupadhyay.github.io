Ghodatabela घोडातबेला

A few lodges and an army and national park checkpost at the point where the forest ends and the valley opens into a broad glacial trough. The name means horse stable. The first clear views of Langtang Lirung come around here.

Also written Ghoda Tabela, Ghora Tabela
