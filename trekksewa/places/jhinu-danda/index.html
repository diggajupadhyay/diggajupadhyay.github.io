Jhinu Danda झिनु डाँडा

A small lodge village on a spur below Chhomrong, known for the hot spring pools beside the Modi Khola about twenty minutes' walk downhill. A long suspension bridge links it to the jeep track.

Also written Jhinu, Jhinudanda
