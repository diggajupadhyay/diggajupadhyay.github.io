Ganja La

A glaciated pass of about 5,120 m south of Kyanjin Gompa that links the Langtang valley with Helambu. It needs a camping crew, rope and ice axe and a guide who knows the route, and snow closes it for much of the year.

Also written Kangja La, Ganja La Pass
