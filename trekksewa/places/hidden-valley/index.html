Hidden Valley हिडन भ्याली

A wide, bare valley at about 5,000 m between French Pass and Dhampus Pass, where every group camps at least one night. It is the coldest and highest camp of the trek, and both ways out cross a pass above 5,200 m. Quoted heights run from 5,000 to 5,200 m.
