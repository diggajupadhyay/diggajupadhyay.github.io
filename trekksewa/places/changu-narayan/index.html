Changu Narayan चाँगुनारायण

A hilltop Newar village around the Changu Narayan temple, a World Heritage monument with stone sculpture going back to the Licchavi period. The ridge trail down from Nagarkot ends here, a short bus ride from Bhaktapur.

Also written Changunarayan
