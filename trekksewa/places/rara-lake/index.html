Rara Lake रारा ताल

Nepal's largest lake, about 10.8 square kilometres and up to 167 m deep, set at 2,990 m among pine, spruce and juniper. The park headquarters and a few simple lodges are on the northern shore.

Also written Rara Tal, Mahendra Tal
