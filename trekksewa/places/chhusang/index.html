Chhusang छुसाङ

A spread-out village where the Narshing Khola joins the Kali Gandaki, facing cliffs pocked with old cave dwellings. Jeeps to and from Jomsom stop here, and it is the usual last night on the way out.

Also written Chuksang, Chusang
