Sekathum

A few lodges where the Ghunsa Khola meets the Tamor, and for now the usual end of the jeep track from Taplejung. The walk up the Ghunsa gorge begins here. Its height is quoted between 1,575 and 1,670 m.

Also written Sukathum, Japantar
