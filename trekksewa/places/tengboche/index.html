Tengboche तेङबोचे

A ridge-top clearing with the largest monastery in Khumbu and a wide view of Ama Dablam, Everest and Lhotse. Lodges fill early in peak season.

Also written Thyangboche

Altitude
3,860 m12,664 ft
Altitude zone
Very high altitude

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
