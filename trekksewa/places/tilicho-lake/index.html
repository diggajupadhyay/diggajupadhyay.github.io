Tilicho Lake तिलिचो ताल

A large glacial lake below the ice wall of Tilicho Peak, usually quoted at 4,919 m. The trail ends at a viewpoint and seasonal tea hut above the south-east shore; the lake is frozen through winter and into spring.

Also written Tilicho Tal
