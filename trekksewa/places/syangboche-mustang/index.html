Syangboche स्याङबोचे

A handful of lodges in a hollow just below the Syangboche La, used as the second night north of Kagbeni. Not to be confused with Syangboche above Namche Bazaar in Khumbu.

Also written Syangmochen, Shyangmochen, Syanboche
