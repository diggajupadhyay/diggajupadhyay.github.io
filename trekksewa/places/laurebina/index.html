Laurebina

A row of lodges on an open ridge at 3,910 m with one of the widest views on the trek: the Annapurnas, Manaslu, Ganesh Himal and Langtang Lirung in a single sweep. Sleeping here splits the height gain between Chandanbari and the lake.

Also written Laurebina Yak, Lauribina, Lauribinayak
