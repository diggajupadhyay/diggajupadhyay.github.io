Chandanbari चन्दनबारी

A lodge village on the forested ridge above Dhunche, also called Sing Gompa after its small monastery. A yak-cheese factory operates here, and it is the usual first night on the way up to Gosaikunda.

Also written Sing Gompa, Shin Gompa, Chandan Bari

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
