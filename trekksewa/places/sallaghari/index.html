Sallaghari

A campsite among pines where the forest begins to thin and the west wall of Dhaulagiri closes in overhead. Crews use slightly different sites along this stretch, so the height you are quoted may be anywhere from 2,800 to 3,100 m.

Also written Salla Ghari, Choriban Khola
