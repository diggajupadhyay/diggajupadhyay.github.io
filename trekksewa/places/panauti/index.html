Panauti पनौती

A small, well-preserved Newar town at the meeting of the Roshi and Punyamati rivers, with the tiered Indreshwar Mahadev temple at its centre. Buses run back to Kathmandu, so it is the usual finish for walks from Dhulikhel and Namobuddha.
