Chogo La

The highest point of the usual Upper Mustang loop, on the western trail between Lo Manthang and Lo Gekar. A long, gradual climb over bare hills without water or shelter. Some maps call it the Marang La and give 4,230 m.

Also written Marang La, Choku La
