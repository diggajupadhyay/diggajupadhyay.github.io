Namobuddha नमोबुद्ध

A Buddhist pilgrimage hill south of Dhulikhel, where a stupa marks the legend of a prince who gave his body to a starving tigress. The large Thrangu Tashi Yangtse monastery on the ridge above takes overnight guests.

Also written Namo Buddha
