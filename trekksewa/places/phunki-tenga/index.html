Phunki Tenga

The low point between Namche and Tengboche: a bridge over the Dudh Koshi, a row of water-driven prayer wheels and a few lunch lodges in the forest. The 600 m climb to the monastery starts on the far side.

Also written Phunke Tenga, Phungi Thanga
