Kyangjuma

A short row of lodges and jewellery stalls on the balcony trail east of Namche, just before the Sanasa junction where the Gokyo and Khumjung paths leave the main Everest trail. A sunny tea stop with Ama Dablam straight ahead.

Also written Kenjoma, Kyanjuma
