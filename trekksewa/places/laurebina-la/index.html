Laurebina La

The 4,610 m pass between Gosaikunda and Helambu, reached past a string of small lakes above the main one. The climb from the lodges is short, but the descent east to Phedi is long, rocky and without shelter, and snow can close it from December to March.

Also written Laurebina Pass, Lauribina La
