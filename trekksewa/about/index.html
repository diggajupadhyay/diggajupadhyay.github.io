About TrekkSewa ट्रेकसेवा

A trekking companion for Nepal, built around two ideas: tell people the truth about a route, and treat the people who work on it fairly.

Why we are building this

सेवा (sewa) means service. Trekking in Nepal depends on service at every step: the guide who notices you have gone quiet, the porter who reaches the lodge before you, the lodge owner who knows whether the pass will go tomorrow. Most of what a trekker needs to know lives in those people's heads, and most of what goes wrong happens when a visitor never gets to hear it.

TrekkSewa puts the basics in one place and says them plainly: how long a route really takes, how high you sleep each night, which permits you need, what tends to go wrong, and what to do when it does.

What is here today

  • 35 route guides with day-by-day stages, altitude profiles and an automatic acclimatization check.
  • 400 places with altitude, facilities and a live forecast.
  • Safety tools that work offline: an altitude sickness check, an acclimatization checker and an emergency toolkit.
  • Planning tools for dates, packing and budget, and a GPX viewer.

What comes next

The second half of the name is the harder half. We are enrolling licensed guides, porters, registered agencies and gear shops so that trekkers can find and contact them directly, with their credentials in view and a shared set of fair-trek standards behind every listing. If that is you, applications are open.

How we handle information

  • Everything is written for TrekkSewa. We do not copy other sites or guidebooks.
  • Where a figure is uncertain we say so, and each trek shows how well established its details are.
  • Fees and rules carry the date we last reviewed them. This content was last reviewed in November 2026.
  • We do not publish trail status we cannot stand behind. A stale report is worse than none.
  • We do not show invented businesses, reviews or statistics. Sample profiles are labelled as samples.

What this is not

TrekkSewa is a planning aid. It is not a guide, a rescue service, a medical service or a substitute for a map and your own judgement. Mountains change faster than websites.
