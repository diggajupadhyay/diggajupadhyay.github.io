Trekking services in Nepal

A trek runs on people: the guide who sets the pace, the porter who carries your bag, the agency that gets the permit, the shop that rents you a sleeping bag. We are building one place to find the good ones.

Enrolling founding partners

Guides

Licensed trekking guides who know the route, the lodges and the signs of altitude illness.

Porters

The people who carry the loads. Hire them directly and fairly, with insurance and proper kit.

Agencies

Registered companies that arrange permits, staff, transport and full trips, and are required for restricted areas.

Gear

Shops in Kathmandu, Pokhara and on the trail that rent and sell what you need.

The fair-trek standards

What every partner agrees to

Based on the International Porter Protection Group's guidelines and on how the better Nepali agencies already work. Partners accept them when they apply.

Checked credentials

Guides are listed with their licence number and agencies with their registration, tourism licence and tax numbers, so you can verify them yourself.

Loads within limits

Porters carry no more than 25 kg including their own kit, and less at altitude or on rough ground.

Wages agreed and paid in full

The daily rate, what it includes and who pays for food and lodging are agreed before the trek and paid without deductions.

Everyone insured

Guides and porters are covered for accident, medical care and evacuation for the whole route, including its highest point.

Dressed for the altitude

Staff have boots, warm clothing, a sleeping place and food suited to the highest and coldest night of the trek.

Nobody sent down alone

A sick or injured guide or porter gets the same care as a client and is never paid off and sent down unaccompanied.

Do you work in trekking?

Guides, porters, agencies and gear shops can apply now. It takes about five minutes, in English or Nepali, and applying is free.
