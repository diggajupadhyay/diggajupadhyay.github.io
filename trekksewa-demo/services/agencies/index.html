Trekking agencies

For restricted areas an agency is the law. Everywhere else a good one saves you days of logistics, and a bad one is the most expensive mistake you can make.

Enrolling founding partners

When you need one

  • Restricted areas. Manaslu, Tsum, Upper Mustang, Nar Phu, Dolpo, Kanchenjunga and the other restricted zones issue permits only through a registered agency, with a licensed guide.
  • TIMS cards. Outside Khumbu the trekker registration card is issued through agencies.
  • Camping routes. Dhaulagiri, Upper Dolpo and similar trips need tents, a kitchen crew and pack animals that only an outfitter can put together.

Questions worth asking

  • What are your company registration, tourism licence and tax numbers?
  • Who exactly will guide us, and can we speak to them before we pay?
  • How are your guides and porters insured, and to what altitude?
  • What is the porter load limit on your trips?
  • What is included, line by line, and what is not?
  • What happens to my money if flights are cancelled or I turn back early?

Warning signs

  • A price far below everyone else's. The saving comes out of staff wages, insurance or your safety margin.
  • Pressure to take a helicopter down for a mild illness. Unnecessary evacuations billed to insurers are a known fraud; call your insurer yourself before agreeing to any flight.
  • Itineraries that skip rest days to look shorter.
  • No written quote and no company papers.

What a legal agency holds Typical, unconfirmed

A registered company with a Department of Tourism trekking agency licence, PAN/VAT tax registration and a Nepal Rastra Bank licence to accept foreign currency; almost all are also TAAN members

Ask for the company registration number, the Department of Tourism licence number and the PAN/VAT number, which a legitimate agency prints on its website and invoices, and look the company up in TAAN's member directory. Only a registered agency can obtain a TIMS card or a restricted-area permit for you, so a company that cannot show its papers cannot legally arrange either.

Permit fees for every region are on the permits page. An agency charges those at cost plus a service fee; ask to see the breakdown.
