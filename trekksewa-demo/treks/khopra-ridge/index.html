Khopra Ridge

A week on forest ridges above Ghandruk and Ghorepani to the community lodge on Khopra Danda at 3,660 m, with an optional long day to Khayer Lake.

Is this trek right for me?

  • The walking

    8 days in all. Most walking days are 4 to 5 hours on foot, and the longest is about 10 hours. Five to seven hour days with real climbs, usually below 4,500 m.

  • The height

    The highest night is at 3,660 m. You will notice the thinner air, but going slowly is usually enough.

  • Where you sleep

    In lodges every night: a simple twin room, a shared bathroom and hot meals in a warm dining room.

  • The help you need

    The rules require a licensed guide on this route. A porter to carry your bag is your choice.

  • What it costs

    A guided trip with a trekking company usually costs USD 500 to 850 a person, going by the prices 10 companies published for 2026.

Overview

Khopra Danda is a grassy spur at 3,660 m that juts out from the south-west flank of Annapurna South, high above the Kali Gandaki gorge. From the lodge on its crest Dhaulagiri fills the western horizon, Annapurna South and Bharha Shikhar rise directly behind, and Tatopani lies more than 2,400 m below. The trek reaches it by linking the forest ridges between Ghandruk, Tadapani and Ghorepani, on trails that see a small share of the traffic on the Poon Hill loop next door.

Several of the lodges on the way are community lodges, built and run by village committees, with the income going to local schools and health posts. They are plain: thin-walled rooms, a stove in the dining room, dal bhat and simple noodle dishes. Private lodges fill the gaps at Tadapani, Dobato and Swanta. There are few beds at each stop, so in October, November and April a guide who can phone ahead is close to essential.

The walking is moderate, mostly on forest paths and open ridge, with a sunrise viewpoint near each end: Muldai above Dobato and Poon Hill above Ghorepani. The exception is the optional day to Khayer Lake, a sacred tarn under Annapurna South. It takes nine or ten hours there and back, with a thousand metres of climbing and no shelter. Treat it as a bonus for a clear day and good legs, not as part of the basic trek.

Highlights

  • Dhaulagiri across the Kali Gandaki gorge from the Khopra lodge
  • Sunrise from Muldai viewpoint, higher and quieter than Poon Hill
  • Community lodges whose income supports village schools
  • The optional high day to the sacred Khayer Lake
  • Poon Hill at dawn on the way out

Moderate: Moderate as far as the ridge at 3,660 m; the optional Khayer Lake day adds 1,000 m of climbing and nine hours or more of walking.

Ways to change it

Reverse direction

Starting at Tikhedhunga and going by Ghorepani and Swanta to Khopra gives gentler height gains. You then return by Bayeli, Dobato, Tadapani and Ghandruk.

Down to Tatopani

From Khopra a very steep trail drops about 2,400 m through Paudwar and Narchyang to the hot springs at Tatopani, with buses to Pokhara through Beni. It is hard on the knees but a good way to finish.

Six days without the lake

Leave out the Khayer Lake day and walk from Khopra Danda to Ghorepani in one long day of seven to eight hours.

Link with Mohare Danda

From Ghorepani a ridge trail leads south-west to the community lodge on Mohare Danda, extending the trek by two or three days.

Permits and guide rules

Guide required. Since April 2023 the Nepal Tourism Board has required foreign trekkers in conservation areas to walk with a licensed guide, though checks on the trail are inconsistent. Here a guide does two specific jobs: reserving beds in the small community lodges, and finding the way on the poorly marked forest and ridge sections between Dobato, Bayeli and Chistibang, where paths fork and cloud is common.

ACAP

Conservation area

Annapurna Conservation Area Permit · National Trust for Nature Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, single entry

Where: Online at NTNC's e-permit site, or in person at the Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, and the Nepal Tourism Board office in Pokhara. A permit issued at a trail checkpost costs double.

The fee includes tax and children under 10 do not need a permit. Paying online by card adds a 2.9% gateway charge. Covers every trek in the Annapurna range and Lower Mustang; Upper Mustang and Nar Phu need a restricted-area permit as well.

TIMS card

Registration

Trekkers' Information Management System card · Nepal Tourism Board and the Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal

Foreign nationalsNPR 2,000person, per trek
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, per trek

Where: Only through a registered trekking agency, which applies in the Nepal Tourism Board's online system with your route and your guide's details. Individual trekkers can no longer buy one over the counter.

Since the revised rules of 31 March 2023 the card is tied to trekking with a licensed guide on the tourism board's listed routes. It is not used in Khumbu, where the municipality's Trek Card took its place in 2017. For restricted areas the published rules pull both ways: TAAN's pages say TIMS applies in every trekking area, restricted ones included, yet also list visitors holding Department of Immigration permits among those who do not need one, and agencies disagree. Your agency, which is compulsory there anyway, will know what the checkposts on your route ask for. Reports from 2025–26 describe patchy checks of the card, especially in Annapurna.

Agency required

Fees and rules change, sometimes mid-season. Treat these as a planning guide and confirm with the Nepal Tourism Board, the Department of Immigration or a registered agency before you travel. Last reviewed 2026-11. All permits and fees

Getting there

Take a jeep from Pokhara to Ghandruk, about three hours, and start walking the same day. The trek comes out at Tikhedhunga on the Ghorepani trail, where jeeps return to Pokhara through Nayapul in two and a half to three hours.

  • Jeep: Pokhara to Ghandruk · 2.5–3 hr

    Or take the local bus to Kimche, an hour's climb below the village.

  • Jeep: Tikhedhunga to Pokhara · 2.5–3 hr

    Jeeps wait at Tikhedhunga and Hile. Otherwise walk on to Birethanti and Nayapul in about three hours.

  • Bus: Tatopani to Pokhara · 5–6 hr

    For the alternative exit down the west side of the ridge through Paudwar.

Hazards on this route

The Khayer Lake day

It is nine or ten hours there and back to about 4,660 m, with no lodge, tea shop or reliable water. Cloud builds from late morning and the path is indistinct in places. Go only with a guide, start before dawn and turn round by noon wherever you are.

Altitude at Dobato and Khopra

You sleep at 3,420 m on the second night and 3,660 m on the fourth, having started from Pokhara at about 800 m. Mild headaches are common at Dobato. If symptoms persist, drop back to Tadapani rather than continuing up.

Cloud and forks in the trail

Between Dobato, Bayeli and Chistibang the route crosses pasture and forest with few signs and many herders' paths. In cloud it is easy to follow the wrong ridge.

Snow on the ridges

From December to March snow can cover the trail above 3,000 m and the community lodges may be shut. Check which lodges are open before leaving Pokhara.

Practical tips

  • Have your guide or agency reserve the community lodges at Chistibang and Khopra Danda; there is only one at each.
  • Carry a sleeping bag. Blankets are provided, but nights on the ridge are below freezing in spring and autumn.
  • Bring cash for the whole trek. The last cash machines are in Pokhara.
  • Carry snacks and a packed lunch on the Bayeli section, where kitchens are slow and choices few.
  • Mobile signal is patchy north of Tadapani, so do not count on it for bookings or navigation.
  • Heights and walking times for the stages north of Tadapani differ between maps and operators; treat the figures here as approximate.

We know of no cash machines on this route. Carry all the rupees you need from Pokhara. Work out how much.

Quick answers

How long does the Khopra Ridge trek take?

Most people take 6–10 days. The itinerary on this page is 8 days.

How high does the Khopra Ridge trek go?

The highest point is Khayer Lake at 4,660 m (15,289 ft). The highest place you sleep is Khopra Danda at 3,660 m.

How difficult is it?

We grade it moderate. Moderate as far as the ridge at 3,660 m; the optional Khayer Lake day adds 1,000 m of climbing and nine hours or more of walking.

Which permits do I need?

ACAP, TIMS card. Fees and where to get each one are listed in the permits section above.

Do I need a guide?
