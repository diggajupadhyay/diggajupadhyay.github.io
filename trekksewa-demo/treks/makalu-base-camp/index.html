Makalu Base Camp

An out-and-back trek from Num across the Arun river, over the Shipton La and up the Barun valley to the foot of Makalu, with simple lodges and very few other trekkers.

Is this trek right for me?

  • The walking

    18 days in all. Most walking days are 5 to 7 hours on foot, and the longest is about 8 hours. Remote, long and high, often with camping, snow or glacier travel.

  • The height

    You sleep as high as 4,870 m. Most people feel the altitude at this height. The plan includes the rest days you need: do not shorten it.

  • Where you sleep

    In lodges on most nights, and in tents on a few: a simple twin room, a shared bathroom and hot meals in a warm dining room.

  • The help you need

    The rules require a licensed guide on this route. A porter to carry your bag is your choice.

  • What it costs

    A guided trip with a trekking company usually costs USD 1,800 to 2,200 a person, going by the prices 9 companies published for 2026.

Overview

Makalu Base Camp covers one of the widest height ranges of any trek in Nepal. You start on the ridge at Num, drop to the Arun river at roughly 700 m, where it is hot enough for rice and bananas, and little more than a week later stand on gravel flats at 4,870 m beneath the 8,485 m pyramid of Makalu. In between come the farming villages of Seduwa and Tashigaon, a forested ridge that gains 1,400 m in a day, a pair of passes above 4,100 m and the cliff-walled Barun valley.

The walking is harder than on the Everest trail. Paths are steep, stony and often wet, stages are fixed by where the few lodges stand, and beyond Tashigaon there are no villages at all. The awkward part is the shape of the route: the passes lie between the upper valley and the way home, so anyone who falls ill at base camp has to climb back over 4,200 m to get out on foot.

Basic lodges now operate at every stop in the main seasons, which is why most parties no longer carry tents, but they are small, plain and sometimes shut. A guide or agency who can send word ahead is worth having. Come with previous experience of altitude, a flexible schedule and a sleeping bag good for well below freezing.

Highlights

  • Makalu's south face from Shershong and base camp
  • The climb from rice terraces by the Arun to moraine at 4,870 m
  • The Shipton La and Keke La, with a small lake between them
  • Cliffs and waterfalls lining the Barun valley around Yangle Kharka
  • The farming villages of Seduwa and Tashigaon
  • A side-on view of Everest and Lhotse from the ridge above base camp

Strenuous: Steep, rough trail with a 1,400 m climb in one day, two passes to cross in both directions and no quick way down from the upper valley.

Ways to change it

Extra acclimatization night

Adding a night at Yangle Kharka or Langmale Kharka is the most useful change you can make to the standard plan.

Camping

Some agencies still run the trek with tents and a kitchen crew. It costs more but removes the worry about closed or full lodges early and late in the season.

Sherpani Col to Khumbu

Beyond base camp a mountaineering route crosses Sherpani Col, West Col and the Amphu Labtsa into the Everest region. It needs ropes, crampons, a climbing guide and an expedition-style crew; it is not a trekking variant.

Permits and guide rules

Guide required. The Nepal Tourism Board has required foreign trekkers to hire a licensed guide since April 2023, although checks on the trail are inconsistent. On this route a guide earns their fee by finding the way over the passes in snow, arranging beds at the few lodges and organising help in a valley with no road, clinic or dependable phone signal.

Makalu Barun National Park

National park

Makalu Barun National Park entry permit · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,500person, single entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person

Where: Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, or the park entry posts at Num and Seduwa on the trail.

Children under 10 are exempt. All three rates match the Nepal Tourism Board's current fee table; whether VAT is added on top is unconfirmed. Agencies also describe a separate charge or permit for Makalu Rural Municipality but quote it inconsistently, from NPR 2,000 to USD 20 a week, so ask your agency what applies on your route.

TIMS card

Registration

Trekkers' Information Management System card · Nepal Tourism Board and the Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal

Foreign nationalsNPR 2,000person, per trek
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, per trek

Where: Only through a registered trekking agency, which applies in the Nepal Tourism Board's online system with your route and your guide's details. Individual trekkers can no longer buy one over the counter.

Since the revised rules of 31 March 2023 the card is tied to trekking with a licensed guide on the tourism board's listed routes. It is not used in Khumbu, where the municipality's Trek Card took its place in 2017. For restricted areas the published rules pull both ways: TAAN's pages say TIMS applies in every trekking area, restricted ones included, yet also list visitors holding Department of Immigration permits among those who do not need one, and agencies disagree. Your agency, which is compulsory there anyway, will know what the checkposts on your route ask for. Reports from 2025–26 describe patchy checks of the card, especially in Annapurna.

Agency required

Fees and rules change, sometimes mid-season. Treat these as a planning guide and confirm with the Nepal Tourism Board, the Department of Immigration or a registered agency before you travel. Last reviewed 2026-11. All permits and fees

Getting there

Fly from Kathmandu to Tumlingtar in the Arun valley, then take a jeep up the ridge through Khandbari and Chichila to Num, where the trail starts. Allow most of a day for the flight and the drive together. Road building in the valley is changing the first stages, so ask in Num how far vehicles currently go.

  • Flight: Kathmandu to Tumlingtar · about 45 min

    Daily in season. Cloud over the middle hills causes delays and the occasional cancellation.

  • Jeep: Tumlingtar to Num · 4–6 hours

    Tarmac as far as Khandbari, rough beyond it, and slower after rain.

  • Bus: Kathmandu to Tumlingtar · a long day or overnight

    Buses and jeeps run to Khandbari and Tumlingtar by road. Tiring, but the fallback when flights are full or grounded.

Hazards on this route

Fast height gain and a blocked retreat

Tashigaon to Khongma gains 1,400 m in a day, and once you are in the Barun valley the only way out on foot is back over the 4,200 m passes. Take the rest day, climb only when free of symptoms and turn round early rather than late.

Snow on the passes

The Shipton La and Keke La can be buried from winter into April and after any storm. The trail disappears and the slopes are steep enough to slide on. Ask at Khongma about conditions before committing, and be ready to wait.

Rockfall beside the Barun

The landslide zone between Dobate and Yangle Kharka drops stones without warning, more so in rain and on warm afternoons. Cross in the morning, spread out, and do not stop for photographs.

Evacuation

There is no road, clinic or dependable phone coverage beyond Tashigaon. Helicopter rescue depends on weather and on getting a message out. Carry evacuation insurance and know how your guide would call for help.

Heat and leeches low down

The Arun crossing is below 800 m. Expect heat and humidity on the first and last stages and, from May to September, leeches as far up as the Khongma ridge.

Practical tips

  • Bring enough rupees from Kathmandu or Khandbari for the whole trek; nothing beyond the road takes cards.
  • Trekking poles are close to essential for the Arun gorge and the Khongma steps.
  • Carry at least two litres of water per person on the Khongma ridge, where springs dry up.
  • Lodges above Tashigaon are few, simple and priced accordingly, and menus shrink to dal bhat, noodles and potatoes. Bring snacks you like.
  • Build two spare days into the plan: one for snow or cloud on the passes, one for the Tumlingtar flight.
  • Agencies disagree about a local municipality fee and a restricted-area permit for this valley. Ask yours in writing which permits your price includes.

We know of no cash machines on this route. Carry all the rupees you need from Kathmandu. Work out how much.

Quick answers

How long does the Makalu Base Camp trek take?

Most people take 16–22 days. The itinerary on this page is 18 days, including 2 days for rest and acclimatization.

How high does the Makalu Base Camp trek go?
