Phoksundo Lake

A short out-and-back trek from the Juphal airstrip up the Suligad gorge to Phoksundo Lake at 3,612 m, staying in simple lodges and needing a Lower Dolpo permit.

Is this trek right for me?

  • The walking

    8 days in all. Most walking days are 5 to 7 hours on foot, and the longest is about 8 hours. Five to seven hour days with real climbs, usually below 4,500 m.

  • The height

    The highest night is at 3,641 m. You will notice the thinner air, but going slowly is usually enough.

  • Where you sleep

    In lodges every night: a simple twin room, a shared bathroom and hot meals in a warm dining room.

  • The help you need

    This is a restricted area. A registered trekking company must arrange your permit, and a licensed guide walks with you.

  • What it costs

    A guided trip with a trekking company usually costs USD 1,450 to 2,350 a person, going by the prices 9 companies published for 2026.

Overview

Phoksundo is the deepest lake in Nepal, about 145 m of blue-green water held behind an old landslide at 3,612 m in Shey Phoksundo National Park. The village of Ringmo, whose people follow the Bon religion, sits on the dam beside it. Reaching it takes three days' walking from the district town of Dunai, up a forested gorge where the Suligad river runs loud beside the trail.

This is the shortest way to see something of Dolpo. The trail is clear and never very high, and plain family-run lodges at Chhepka, Rechi, Chunuwar and Ringmo mean you do not need a camping crew. The effort goes into getting there: two flights, the second into a hillside airstrip that closes whenever wind or cloud builds, and a restricted-area permit that has to be arranged through a registered agency with a licensed guide.

Come for the lake, the waterfall below it and the quiet. Facilities are thin: no cash machines to rely on after Nepalgunj, patchy phone signal, and food limited to what mules and porters carry in. In May and June many villagers are away on the high pastures collecting yarsagumba, and some lodges are locked.

Highlights

  • Phoksundo Lake, Nepal's deepest, changing colour through the day
  • Ringmo village and the Bon monastery of Tshowa on the shore
  • The 167 m waterfall where the lake's outflow drops into the gorge
  • The cliff trail above the lake's western shore
  • Pine, cedar and birch forest in the Suligad gorge
  • A first look at Dolpo's Tibetan-influenced culture without a camping crew

Moderate: Clear trails and modest altitude, but long stages in a gorge, simple lodges and a trailhead reached only by weather-dependent flights.

Ways to change it

Straight to Chhepka

Skip the night in Dunai by walking or taking a jeep from Juphal to the park gate at Suligad and continuing up the gorge the same day. It saves a day if your agency has already dealt with the paperwork.

Lower Dolpo circuit

A camping loop of about two weeks links Ringmo with Dho Tarap over the Baga La and Numa La, both above 5,000 m, using the Tarap gorge between Dho and Dunai.

On to Shey Gompa

With the Upper Dolpo permit and a camping crew you can carry on along the lake and over the Kang La to Shey Gompa; see the Upper Dolpo route.

Permits and guide rules

Guide required. Lower Dolpo is a restricted area, so the permit comes only through a registered trekking agency and you must walk with a licensed guide, whether you are alone or in a group. On the ground, a guide who knows the lodge owners makes the difference between a bed and a meal and a locked door, especially in the yarsagumba season when many families are away.

Shey Phoksundo National Park

National park

Shey Phoksundo National Park entry permit · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,500person, single entry

Where: Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, or the park entry post on the trail in from Juphal.

The Nepal Tourism Board page gives NPR 3,000 and NPR 1,500 per entry; whether VAT is added on top is unconfirmed, as for the other national parks. Park headquarters are at Sumduwa. The Dolpo restricted-area permits are separate.

Lower Dolpo RAP

Restricted area

Lower Dolpo restricted area permit · Department of Immigration

All foreign nationalsUSD 20person per week, then USD 5 a day

Where: Department of Immigration in Kathmandu, through a registered trekking agency only.

Since 22 March 2026 the permit can be issued to a single trekker; before that the minimum was two. You still have to book through a registered agency and walk with a licensed guide, with at most seven trekkers per guide. The Shey Phoksundo park permit is extra.

Getting there

Fly from Kathmandu to Nepalgunj on the plains near the Indian border, spend the night, and take the early flight to Juphal in Dolpa next morning. Juphal flights run only in calm, clear weather, so a delay of a day or more at either end is common. A rough road now links Dunai with the lowlands, but it is slow and often blocked.

  • Flight: Kathmandu to Nepalgunj · about 1 hour

    Several flights a day. Buses take ten to twelve hours.

  • Flight: Nepalgunj to Juphal · about 40 min

    Small aircraft, early morning only, and frequently delayed or cancelled by wind and cloud.

  • Jeep: Nepalgunj to Dunai · about 2 days

    The Bheri valley road through Jajarkot is rough, mostly a dry-season route and prone to landslides. A fallback rather than a plan.

Hazards on this route

Flight delays at Juphal

The airstrip is short, sloping and exposed to wind, so flights are cancelled often and backlogs build. Carry extra cash for unplanned nights and keep at least two spare days in your overall schedule.

The gorge trail

Sections between Chhepka and Chunuwar are narrow, cut into steep slopes and damaged by landslides most summers, and some bridges are temporary log crossings. Take care in rain, and stand on the uphill side when mule or yak trains pass.

Altitude at the lake

You sleep at 3,641 m four days after leaving the plains. Most people notice no more than a mild headache, but drink well, climb slowly on the day from Chunuwar and do not ignore symptoms that get worse.

Thin services

There is no hospital beyond Dunai, phone coverage is patchy and a helicopter needs good weather. A proper first-aid kit and evacuation insurance belong on the kit list here.

Practical tips

  • Take all your cash from Kathmandu or Nepalgunj. Do not count on finding a working cash machine in Dolpa.
  • Lodges are simple rooms around a kitchen fire. Bring a sleeping bag, and expect dal bhat, noodles, potatoes and Tibetan bread rather than a menu.
  • Nepalgunj is hot for most of the year; pack one set of light clothes for the night there.
  • Walk round Bon shrines and mani walls anticlockwise, the opposite of Buddhist custom, and ask before taking photographs inside Tshowa monastery.
  • Water in the gorge comes from the river and from side streams used by livestock. Treat all of it.
  • If you have a spare day, spend it at the lake rather than hurrying down. Early morning and evening light are the reason to come.

We know of no cash machines on this route. Carry all the rupees you need from Nepalgunj. Work out how much.

Quick answers

How long does the Phoksundo Lake trek take?

Most people take 7–11 days. The itinerary on this page is 8 days, including 1 day for rest and acclimatization.

How high does the Phoksundo Lake trek go?

The highest point is Phoksundo Lake Viewpoint at 4,040 m (13,255 ft). The highest place you sleep is Ringmo at 3,641 m.

How difficult is it?

We grade it moderate. Clear trails and modest altitude, but long stages in a gorge, simple lodges and a trailhead reached only by weather-dependent flights.

Which permits do I need?

Shey Phoksundo National Park, Lower Dolpo RAP. Fees and where to get each one are listed in the permits section above.

Do I need a guide?

Lower Dolpo is a restricted area, so the permit comes only through a registered trekking agency and you must walk with a licensed guide, whether you are alone or in a group. On the ground, a guide who knows the lodge owners makes the difference between a bed and a meal and a locked door, especially in the yarsagumba season when many families are away.

When is the best time to go?

Apr–May, Sep–Oct are best. Spring and autumn give the clearest days, and October is the pick. Summer is workable because Dolpo gets far less rain than the central hills, though the gorge is wetter than the country beyond the lake and flights are disrupted more often. November is cold but usually clear. From December to March lodges close, most of Ringmo moves down the valley and snow can block the upper trail.
