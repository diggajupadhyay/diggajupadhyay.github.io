Dhading Besi धादिङबेसी

The district headquarters of Dhading, a low, warm bazaar town where the jeep road from Ruby Valley meets the buses for Kathmandu. Its link to the Prithvi Highway at Malekhu lost a bridge in the August 2026 flood; ask which road is in use.
