AnnapurnaVillage

Landruk लान्द्रुक

A Gurung village on the east side of the Modi Khola, facing Ghandruk across the valley. It lies on the old Dhampus route to Annapurna Base Camp and on one of the ways down from the Mardi Himal ridge.
