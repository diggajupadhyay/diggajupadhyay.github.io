Gosaikunda गोसाइँकुण्ड

A dark alpine lake at 4,380 m, sacred to Shiva and the goal of a large pilgrimage at the Janai Purnima full moon in August. Lodges line the slope above its northern shore; Bhairav Kunda and Saraswati Kunda lie just below.

Also written Gosainkunda, Gosaikunda Lake

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
