Mohare Danda मोहरे डाँडा

A forested hilltop with a community lodge and a clearing that looks out to Dhaulagiri, the Annapurnas and Machhapuchhre. It is slightly higher than Poon Hill and sees a fraction of the visitors.

Also written Mohare Hill
