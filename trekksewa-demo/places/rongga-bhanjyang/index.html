Rongga Bhanjyang

A saddle at about 2,190 m on the ridge west of Syabrubesi, two hours' steep climb above the town. The first walking day of the Tamang Heritage Trail crosses it, with a view of Ganesh Himal and down over the terraces of Goljung.

Also written Bahun Danda Pass
