AnnapurnaVillage

Ghorepani घोरेपानी

A large lodge village on a forested saddle below Poon Hill, where the trails from Nayapul, Tatopani and Tadapani meet. Nearly everyone here is up before dawn for the walk to the viewpoint.

Also written Ghodepani

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
