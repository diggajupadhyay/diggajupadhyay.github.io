Nagarkot नगरकोट

A ridge of hotels on the eastern rim of the Kathmandu Valley, built around a sunrise view that reaches from the Annapurnas to Everest on the clearest days. The usual 2,175 m figure is the view-tower hill; most hotels sit a little lower.
