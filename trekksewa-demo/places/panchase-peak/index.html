Panchase Peak पञ्चासे

The forested top of the hill at the head of Phewa Lake, with a small temple and a clearing that looks out from Dhaulagiri to Manaslu. The last hour from the Bhanjyang is all stone steps, usually climbed before dawn.

Also written Panchase Hill, Panchase Lekh, Panchase Danda

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
