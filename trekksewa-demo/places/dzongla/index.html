Dzongla जोङ्ला

A few lodges on a shelf under the north face of Cholatse, used the night before or after the Cho La. Beds are limited and run short when bad weather has held groups back for a day.

Also written Dzonglha, Zongla

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
