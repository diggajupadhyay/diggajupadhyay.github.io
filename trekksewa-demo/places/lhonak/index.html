Eastern NepalLodge settlement

Lhonak

Basic stone lodges on a cold, sandy plain beside the Kanchenjunga Glacier, and the last place most people sleep before the north base camp. Expect wind, hard frost and very simple food.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
