Namche Bazaar नाम्चे बजार

The Sherpa capital, set in a steep horseshoe bowl above the Bhote Koshi. It is the last place with real shops, cash machines and a pharmacy, and the standard first acclimatization stop.

Also written Namche

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
