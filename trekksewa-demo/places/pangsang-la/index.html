Pangsang La

A broad grassy saddle at about 3,842 m, the high point of the Ruby Valley trek, with Ganesh Himal to the north and Manaslu and the Langtang peaks on either hand. A simple lodge stands at the pass, and a rough road now reaches it from the Dhading side.

Also written Pangsang Pass, Pangsang Bhanjyang, Pangsang Kharka
