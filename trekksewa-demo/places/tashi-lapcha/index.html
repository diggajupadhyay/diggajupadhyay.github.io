RolwalingPass

Tashi Lapcha टासी लाप्चा

The glaciated pass at the head of the Rolwaling valley that leads over to Thame in Khumbu. It involves glacier travel, slopes exposed to rockfall and at least two high camps, and is a mountaineering route, not a trek.

Also written Tesi Lapcha, Trashi Labtsa, Tashi Lapcha Pass
