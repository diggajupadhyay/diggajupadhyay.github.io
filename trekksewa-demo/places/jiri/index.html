EverestTown

Jiri जिरी

A bazaar town in Dolakha district, about 190 km by road from Kathmandu. From the 1980s, when the Swiss-built road reached it, until Lukla flights became routine, the walk to Everest began here. Buses now carry on to Shivalaya.
