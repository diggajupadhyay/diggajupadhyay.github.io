Kalikasthan

A ridge-top farming village north-east of Pokhara and the first night on the Royal Trek. Itineraries print 1,370 m; the houses stand lower, at about 1,200 to 1,250 m, and the ridge behind them rises to about 1,400 m.

Also written Kalikathan, Kalika
