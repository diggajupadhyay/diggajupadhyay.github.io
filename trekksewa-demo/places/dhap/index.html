Dhap ढाप

A roadside bazaar on the Okhaldhunga to Salleri road at the southern edge of Solukhumbu, and the usual start of the Pikey Peak trek. Jeeps from Kathmandu take most of a day to reach it.

Also written Dhap Bazaar

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
