Melamchi Gaun मेलम्ची गाउँ

A Hyolmo village on a sunny terrace beneath Tharepati, with a gompa, apple orchards and a meditation cave linked to Guru Rinpoche. It faces Tarkeghyang across the deep valley of the Melamchi Khola.

Also written Melamchighyang, Melamchi Ghyang

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
