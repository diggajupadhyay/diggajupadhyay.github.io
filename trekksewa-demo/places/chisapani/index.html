Chisapani चिसापानी

A cluster of hotels on a windy shoulder just north of the Shivapuri ridge, known for its dawn skyline from Ganesh Himal and Langtang Lirung east to Dorje Lakpa. Maps quote anything from 2,140 to 2,215 m for it.

Tip. It is colder here than the height suggests. Bring a warm layer for the evening and be outside before sunrise, when the air is clearest.
