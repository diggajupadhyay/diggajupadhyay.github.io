Samdo साम्दो

The last permanent village before the Larkya La, settled by families who crossed from Tibet after 1959. Trails north from here lead to the old trade passes on the border.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
