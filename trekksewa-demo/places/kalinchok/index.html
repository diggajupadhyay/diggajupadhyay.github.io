Kalinchok कालिञ्चोक

A hilltop shrine to Kalinchok Bhagwati above the lodge village of Kuri, north of Charikot. A cable car and a stone staircase lead to the top, which draws Nepali visitors for winter snow and a long mountain skyline.

Also written Kalinchowk, Kalinchok Bhagwati
