Phoksundo Khola Camp

A camping ground among birch and willow on the flood plain where the Phoksundo Khola enters the head of the lake. It is the first night out from Ringmo on the way to Shey.

Altitude
3,630 m11,909 ft
Altitude zone
Very high altitude

64%of sea level, roughly

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
