Chalish

A village of Gurung, Ghale and Kami families a short walk below Shertung, and the usual second homestay night in Ruby Valley. Hosts can point the way to a hot spring two to three hours off; ask whether the path is open.

Also written Chalish Gaun, Chalish Gaon
