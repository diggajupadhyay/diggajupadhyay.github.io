Dongang

Two or three simple lodges in a forest clearing beside the Rolwaling Khola, the only practical overnight stop between Simigaon and Beding. Expect shared rooms and dal bhat.

Also written Donggang, Dong Gang

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
