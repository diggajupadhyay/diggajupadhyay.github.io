ManasluPass

Larkya La लार्के पास

The high point of the Manaslu Circuit, a long, gradual crossing over moraine and not a sharp notch. The sign at the top reads 5,106 m; some maps give 5,160 m. The west side drops steeply towards Bhimtang.

Also written Larke La, Larke Pass, Larkya Pass

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.

Weather at Larkya La

The forecast loads when you are online.

Tip. Be over the top by late morning. Wind and cloud build after midday and there is almost no shelter on either side.
