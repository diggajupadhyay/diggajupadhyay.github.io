EverestVillage

Pangboche पाङबोचे

The highest year-round village on the Everest trail, with the oldest monastery in Khumbu in its upper half. The starting point for the side trip to Ama Dablam Base Camp.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
