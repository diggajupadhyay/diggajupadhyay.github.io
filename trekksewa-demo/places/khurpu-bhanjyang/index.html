Khurpu Bhanjyang

The forested pass between Gatlang and Somdang, at about 3,720 m; itineraries quote anything from 3,610 to 3,730 m. It is the first of the two passes on the Ruby Valley trek, and a climb of nearly 1,500 m from Gatlang.

Also written Khurpudanda Pass, Khurpu Pass, Khurphu Bhanjyang
