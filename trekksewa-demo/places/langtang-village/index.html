LangtangVillage

Langtang Village लाङटाङ

The valley's main village. The old settlement was buried by an avalanche and landslide set off by the earthquake of 25 April 2015, which killed more than 240 people here; the rebuilt village and its lodges stand just up-valley of the debris.
