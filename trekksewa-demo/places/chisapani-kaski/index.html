Chisapani

A hill village east of Rupa Lake and the last night on the Royal Trek. A small temple on the knoll above looks over the lakes to the Annapurna range. Printed heights of 1,550 to 1,630 m are too high; maps show about 1,300 m.

Also written Chisopani, Chisapani (Kaski), Chisapani Danda
