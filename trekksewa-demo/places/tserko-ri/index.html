Tserko Ri

A 4,984 m walk-up summit east of Kyanjin Gompa, often advertised as 5,033 m. The climb needs no scrambling, but it gains more than 1,100 m and takes most of a day; the top gives a full circle of peaks from Langtang Lirung round to Gangchempo and Dorje Lakpa.

Also written Tsergo Ri, Cherko Ri
