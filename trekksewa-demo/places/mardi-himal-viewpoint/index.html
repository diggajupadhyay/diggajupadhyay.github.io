Mardi Himal Upper Viewpoint

A prayer-flagged knoll on the narrow ridge above High Camp where most trekkers stop. Machhapuchhre is only a few kilometres away, with Annapurna South and Hiunchuli across the Modi Khola.

Also written Upper Viewpoint, Mardi Himal View Point
