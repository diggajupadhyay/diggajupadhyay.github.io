Dhulikhel Kali Temple

A small hilltop temple at about 1,700 m on the south-east edge of Dhulikhel, reached by a long flight of stone steps, often called the thousand steps, past a gilded Buddha statue. The walk to Namobuddha crosses the hill, and its terrace faces the peaks.

Also written Kali Temple, Kali Mandir, Devisthan
