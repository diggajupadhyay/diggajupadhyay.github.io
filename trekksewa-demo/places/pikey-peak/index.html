Pikey Peak

The highest point of the lower Solu ridges, a grassy twin summit strung with prayer flags. On a clear morning the view runs from Gaurishankar and Numbur past Everest and Makalu to Kanchenjunga on the eastern horizon.

Also written Pike Peak, Pikey Danda
