Cheram

A cluster of lodges in the Simbuwa Khola valley, also spelled Tseram, where the pass route from Ghunsa meets the trail to the south base camp. Forest starts again just below it.

Also written Tseram

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
