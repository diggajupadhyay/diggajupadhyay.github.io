Dingboche दिङबोचे

A sunny summer settlement of stone-walled potato fields in the Imja valley and the usual second acclimatization stop. Sunnier and a little less windy than Pheriche next door.

Altitude
4,410 m14,469 ft
Altitude zone

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
