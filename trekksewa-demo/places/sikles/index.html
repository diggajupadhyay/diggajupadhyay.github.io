Sikles सिक्लेस

One of the largest Gurung villages in Nepal: several hundred stone houses on a spur above the Madi Khola, facing Lamjung Himal and Annapurna II. A rough road reaches it from Pokhara, and families take guests in homestays.

Also written Siklis, Siklesh
