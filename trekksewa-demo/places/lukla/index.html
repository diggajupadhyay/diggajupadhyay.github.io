Lukla लुक्ला

The gateway to Khumbu, built around the short, sloping runway of Tenzing-Hillary Airport. Guides and porters meet their groups here and the trail north begins at the edge of the village.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
