Chutok La

A low forested pass between Paiya and Surke and the last climb of the walk-in before the trail drops into the valley below Lukla. The path is narrow and shared with mule trains.

Also written Chutok Pass

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.

Lodges also close for the winter higher up. You cannot book a bed through us yet: in October, November, March and April ask your guide or trekking company to phone ahead. Plan a trek to see the stops night by night.

Tip. Stand on the uphill side when mules pass; the drop on the outer edge is steep.
