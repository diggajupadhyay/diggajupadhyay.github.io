Kyanjin Ri

The ridge directly behind Kyanjin Gompa. Prayer flags on a lower shoulder at about 4,300 m are often taken for the top; the true summit at 4,773 m is another hour up a steep, exposed crest and looks straight onto the Lirung glacier.

Also written Kyangjin Ri
