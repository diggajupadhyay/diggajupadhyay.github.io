Oktang

A chorten hung with prayer flags on the moraine beside the Yalung Glacier, at about 4,730 m. It is as far as trekkers go on the south side and looks up the glacier to Kanchenjunga's south-west face.

Also written Kanchenjunga South Base Camp, Oktang viewpoint
