Thorong Phedi थोरङ फेदी

The lodges at the foot of the climb to the Thorong La, set under cliffs beside the river. Most trekkers sleep here and start for the pass between four and five in the morning.

Also written Thorung Phedi

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
