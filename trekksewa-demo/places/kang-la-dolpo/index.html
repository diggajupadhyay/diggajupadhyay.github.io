Kang La (Dolpo)

The pass between the Phoksundo and Shey valleys, about 5,350 m and also known as the Nangdalo La. The southern side is steep slate scree, often under snow. It is the high point of the Upper Dolpo route.

Also written Nangdalo La, Nangdala La
