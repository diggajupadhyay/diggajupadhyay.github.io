Gokyo गोक्यो

A lodge village on the shore of the third Gokyo lake, Dudh Pokhari, with the Ngozumpa Glacier hidden behind the moraine at its back. It is the base for Gokyo Ri, the upper lakes, the Renjo La and the Cho La.
