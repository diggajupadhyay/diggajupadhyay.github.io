Khayer Lake

A small sacred lake with a shrine below the west flank of Annapurna South, reached by a long day walk from Khopra Danda. Its height is quoted anywhere from 4,500 to 4,660 m and there is no shelter on the way.

Also written Khayar Tal, Khayer Barahi
