Italian Base Camp इटालियन बेस क्याम्प

A grassy shelf under the west face of Dhaulagiri I and the last comfortable campsite before the glacier. Parties normally spend two nights here to acclimatize, and it is the usual point for a helicopter pick-up if someone has to go out.
