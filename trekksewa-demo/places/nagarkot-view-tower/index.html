Nagarkot View Tower

A lookout tower on the highest point of the Nagarkot ridge, about 4 km south of the bazaar, quoted at 2,164 or 2,175 m. At dawn it has a full sweep of the northern skyline, and the walk to Dhulikhel starts down through the forest from here.

Also written Nagarkot Tower, Nagarkot Lookout Tower
