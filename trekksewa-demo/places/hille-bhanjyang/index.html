Hille Bhanjyang

A camp in a notch of the ridge at about 3,400 m, between Pauwa Bas and Nasim Pati, where the forest thins to rhododendron and pasture. Groups pitch tents here; some agency pages mention a simple shelter, which we could not confirm.
