AnnapurnaVillage

Ngawal ङावल

A large traditional village on the high route between Pisang and Manang, and the first village on the descent from the Kang La. It is higher than Manang, so a night here helps with acclimatization.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
