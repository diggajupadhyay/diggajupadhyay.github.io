Nasim Pati

Pilgrim shelters and a few seasonal teahouses on the ridge at about 3,700 m, where the trail from Bhotang meets the one from Chautara. It is the last night before the lakes, three to four hours above.

Also written Nasimpati, Narsim Pati, Nosyampati

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
