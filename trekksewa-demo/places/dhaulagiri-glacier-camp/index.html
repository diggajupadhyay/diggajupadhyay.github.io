Glacier Camp

A cramped campsite on the rubble-covered Chhonbardan Glacier, in the gorge between Dhaulagiri I and Tsaurabong Peak. Tent platforms are levelled on ice and stones, and rockfall from both walls can be heard through the night.

Also written Dhaulagiri Glacier Camp
