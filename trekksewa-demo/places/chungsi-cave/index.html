Chungsi Cave

A cave shrine with natural rock formations revered as self-arisen chortens and linked to Guru Rinpoche. It lies deep in a canyon on the walking-only trail between Samar and Syangboche, well away from the jeep road.

Also written Rangchyung Chorten, Ranchung Cave
