ManasluBase camp

Manaslu Base Camp मनास्लु आधार शिविर

The expedition base on a moraine shelf beside the Manaslu glacier, a steep climb of about 1,270 m from Sama Gaun. Tents fill it in September and October. There and back is a full, hard day.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
