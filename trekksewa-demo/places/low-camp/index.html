Low Camp

Lodges at the upper edge of the forest on the Mardi Himal ridge, with the first close view of Machhapuchhre up the valley. The steep trail down to Siding leaves the ridge here.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
