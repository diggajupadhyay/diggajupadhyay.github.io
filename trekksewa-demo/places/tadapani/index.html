Tadapani ताडापानी

A cluster of lodges in a forest clearing on the ridge between Ghorepani and Ghandruk. On a clear morning Annapurna South and Machhapuchhre stand right in front of the dining rooms.

Lodges also close for the winter higher up. You cannot book a bed through us yet: in October, November, March and April ask your guide or trekking company to phone ahead.
