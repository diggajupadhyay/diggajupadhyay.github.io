Chichemle

A clearing with a basic lodge roughly halfway up the ridge between Hile Kharka and Khumai Danda. Most groups stop for lunch, and slower walkers can spend an extra night. Sources give heights from 2,400 to 2,650 m.

Also written Chichemle Kharka, Chichimle
