Thini ठिनी

An old Thakali village on a terrace across the river from Jomsom, older than the town itself. It makes a short walk away from the road and is on the way to Dhumba Lake.

Also written Thinigaon, Thini Gaon

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
