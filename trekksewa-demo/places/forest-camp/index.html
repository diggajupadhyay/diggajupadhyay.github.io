Forest Camp

Basic lodges in a clearing in dense oak and rhododendron forest on the Mardi Himal ridge. There are no views; it is simply the first place to stop on the long forest section.

Also written Kokar

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
