EverestVillage

Khumjung खुम्जुङ

A large Sherpa farming village beneath Khumbila, home to the school Edmund Hillary founded in 1961 and a monastery. Quieter than Namche and a good detour on the acclimatization day.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
