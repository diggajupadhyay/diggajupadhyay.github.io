Kavre Bhanjyang काभ्रे भञ्ज्याङ

A roadside village in a saddle on the BP Highway, a few kilometres south-east of Dhulikhel. The path from the Kali temple comes down to the road here and the walking route to Namobuddha leaves it, so day walkers often start here by bus to skip the steps.
