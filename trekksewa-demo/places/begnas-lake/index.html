Begnas Lake बेगनास ताल

The second largest lake of the Pokhara valley, a short bus ride east of the city and much quieter than Phewa. The bazaar and bus stand lie below the dam at its south-western corner, where rowing boats are for hire.

Also written Begnas Tal, Begnas Bazaar
