AnnapurnaVillage

Nar नार

A large farming village on a sunny shelf below Pisang Peak, livelier than Phu, with barley fields, several small monasteries and a few lodges. It is the last village before the Kang La.

Also written Nar Gaon, Nargaon

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
