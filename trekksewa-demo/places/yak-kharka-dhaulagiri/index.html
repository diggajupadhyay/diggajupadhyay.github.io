Yak Kharka याक खर्क

A herders' pasture on the hillside above Marpha and the first camp below the snowline after Dhampus Pass. Reaching it means more than 1,500 m of descent from the pass. Not the Yak Kharka on the Annapurna Circuit.

Also written Yak Kharka (Marpha)
