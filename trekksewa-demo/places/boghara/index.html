AnnapurnaVillage

Boghara बगर

The last permanent village, a few terraced farms on a steep hillside above the Myagdi Khola. The path to it crosses exposed grass slopes and rock steps. From here to Marpha there are only seasonal huts and campsites.

Also written Bagar, Bagara
