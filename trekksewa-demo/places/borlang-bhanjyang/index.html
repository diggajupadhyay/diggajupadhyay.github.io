Borlang Bhanjyang

The forested saddle at about 2,420 m where the trail from Sundarijal crosses the Shivapuri ridge, and the highest point on the walk to Chisapani. The first mountain views of the route open on the descent beyond it.

Also written Burlang Bhanjyang
