EverestPass

Cho La चो ला

The pass between the Khumbu and Gokyo valleys. The east side crosses a short stretch of snow-covered glacier and the west side is a steep slope of loose rock, so it needs more care than its height alone suggests.

Also written Cho La Pass, Chola Pass
