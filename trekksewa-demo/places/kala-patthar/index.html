Kala Patthar काला पत्थर

A dark rocky shoulder of Pumori above Gorak Shep and the classic viewpoint for Everest, Nuptse and the Khumbu Icefall. Usually quoted at 5,545 m; surveys of the upper rocks put them about 100 m higher.
