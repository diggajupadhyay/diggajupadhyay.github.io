Luri Gompa लुरी गुम्बा

A cave temple set high in a sandstone pinnacle about two hours' walk above Yara. Inside, a polished white chorten is surrounded by finely painted murals usually dated to the 13th or 14th century.

Also written Luri Cave Monastery
