AnnapurnaBase camp

Machhapuchhre Base Camp माछापुच्छ्रे आधार शिविर

A group of lodges at the entrance to the Annapurna Sanctuary, under the north-west face of Machhapuchhre. The peak is sacred and closed to climbers, so the name is a courtesy and no expedition uses it.

Also written MBC, Machapuchare Base Camp

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.

Lodges also close for the winter higher up. You cannot book a bed through us yet: in October, November, March and April ask your guide or trekking company to phone ahead.
