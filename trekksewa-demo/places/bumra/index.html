Bumra

A village above the Sinja Khola on the direct trail between Jumla and Rara, with a couple of basic lodges. It is the usual overnight stop between the two passes.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
