Eastern NepalPass

Sinion La

The first pass south of the Sele La huts, a rocky notch at about 4,440 m reached by a rough rising traverse. A few sources put it nearer 4,650 m.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
