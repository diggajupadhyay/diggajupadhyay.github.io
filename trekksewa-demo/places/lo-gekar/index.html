Lo Gekar घर गुम्बा

A small Nyingma monastery in a side valley west of Tsarang, better known as Ghar Gompa. Tradition says Guru Rinpoche founded it in the 8th century, before Samye in Tibet, which would make it one of the oldest Tibetan Buddhist temples anywhere.

Also written Ghar Gompa, Lo Ghyakar
