Magin Goth

A few simple lodges in a forest clearing at about 3,285 m, between Kutumsang and Tharepati. Breaking the climb here keeps the day's height gain to roughly 800 m and makes the night at Tharepati easier.

Also written Mangengoth, Mangen Goth
