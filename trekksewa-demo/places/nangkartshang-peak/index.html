Nangkartshang Peak

The steep ridge directly above Dingboche, climbed as an acclimatization hike. From the prayer flags you look across to Makalu, Lhotse, Island Peak and Ama Dablam.

Also written Nagarjun Hill, Nangkar Tshang

Altitude
5,083 m16,677 ft
Altitude zone
Very high altitude
Oxygen per breath
53%of sea level, roughly
Water boils at

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
