Lo Manthang लोमन्थाङ

The walled capital of the former kingdom of Lo. Inside the earthen walls are the old royal palace and the Jampa, Thubchen and Chode temples, with wall paintings dating from the 15th century. Most lodges stand just outside the gate.

Also written Lo-Manthang, Monthang
