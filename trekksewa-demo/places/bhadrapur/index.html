Bhadrapur भद्रपुर

A Terai town in Jhapa whose airport, often listed as Chandragadhi, is the air gateway to Kanchenjunga and the Ilam tea hills. Flights from Kathmandu take about 45 minutes and the drive to Taplejung starts here.
