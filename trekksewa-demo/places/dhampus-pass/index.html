Dhampus Pass धम्पुस पास

The exit from Hidden Valley towards the Kali Gandaki, between Tukuche Peak and Dhampus Peak. The climb to it is short, but the far side is a long traverse above 5,000 m across slopes that avalanche after fresh snow.

Also written Thapa Pass, Dhampus La
