Panch Pokhari पाँच पोखरी

Five small lakes in a grassy bowl at about 4,100 m in Langtang National Park, sacred to Shiva and the goal of a large pilgrimage at the Janai Purnima full moon in August. A small shrine and a pilgrims' shelter stand by the water.

Also written Paanch Pokhari, Panchpokhari, Panch Pokhari Lakes
