Humde

A village beside Manang's airstrip on the valley floor. Scheduled flights are rare, but it sits on the road and the low route, and jeeps down the valley can be arranged here.

Also written Hongde, Manang Airport

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
