AnnapurnaVillage

Phu फु

A fortified hilltop village of stacked stone houses at the head of the Phu Khola, home to a few dozen families of Tibetan origin. Tashi Lhakhang monastery sits on the hill across the river.

Also written Phu Gaon, Phugaon
