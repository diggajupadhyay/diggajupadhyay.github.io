Mundu

A small stone village half an hour beyond Langtang village, with a few quieter lodges among walled fields. Long mani walls line the trail on either side of it.

Altitude
3,543 m11,624 ft
Altitude zone

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
