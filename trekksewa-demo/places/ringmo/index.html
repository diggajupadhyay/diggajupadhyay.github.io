Ringmo

A Bon village of flat-roofed stone houses on the old landslide dam that holds back Phoksundo Lake. It has the only lodges at the lake and a national park post.

Also written Tsho, Rigmo

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
