Dhulikhel धुलिखेल

An old Newar trading town on the Araniko Highway, about 30 km east of Kathmandu, with a wide northern skyline from the hill above its bazaar. It makes a natural end to the rim walk from Nagarkot and the start of the day hike to Namobuddha.
