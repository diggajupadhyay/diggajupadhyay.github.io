Deboche देबोचे

A quiet group of lodges in rhododendron and birch forest twenty minutes below Tengboche, next to a small nunnery. Groups sleep here when Tengboche is full, or for a less windy night.

Also written Debuche, Dibuche

Altitude
3,820 m12,533 ft
Altitude zone
Very high altitude

62%of sea level, roughly

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
