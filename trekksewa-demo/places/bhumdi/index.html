Bhumdi

A farming village along the ridge south-west of Phewa Lake, with a few family lodges that look north to Machhapuchhre and the Annapurnas. It is the usual first night on the Panchase walk, and a road reaches it from Pokhara.

Also written Bumdi, Pumdi Bhumdi
