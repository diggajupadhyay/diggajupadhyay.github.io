Kongma La कोङ्मा ला

The highest and roughest of the three passes, a rocky notch above a group of small lakes between Chhukung and Lobuche. There is no lodge, shelter or reliable water between the two villages.

Also written Kongma La Pass

Extreme altitude. The body cannot fully adapt. Keep visits short. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
