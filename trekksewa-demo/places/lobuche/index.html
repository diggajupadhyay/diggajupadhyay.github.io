Lobuche लोबुचे

A cold cluster of lodges beside the Khumbu Glacier moraine and the last overnight stop before Gorak Shep. Nights here are where many trekkers first sleep badly.

Altitude
4,940 m16,207 ft
Altitude zone

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
