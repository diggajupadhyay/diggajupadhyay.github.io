Dhaulagiri Base Camp धौलागिरि आधार शिविर

The expedition base on the upper Chhonbardan Glacier, directly beneath the north face of Dhaulagiri I, with Tukuche Peak and the icefall to the north-east col across the ice. The second acclimatization stop before the passes.

Altitude
4,740 m15,551 ft
Altitude zone
Very high altitude

55%of sea level, roughly

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
