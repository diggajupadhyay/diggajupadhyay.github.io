Dharamsala धर्मशाला

The last shelter before the Larkya La: a few stone huts and rows of tents with shared rooms, open only in the trekking seasons. It is cold, basic and often full in October.

Also written Larkya Phedi, Larke Phedi, Dharmashala

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
