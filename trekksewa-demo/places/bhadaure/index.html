Bhadaure

A village on the ridge between Panchase and the Baglung highway, where the forest gives way to terraced fields. It has a few simple lodges, and a dirt road with local buses and jeeps to Pokhara by way of Kande.

Also written Bhadaure Deurali, Bhadaure Tamagi
