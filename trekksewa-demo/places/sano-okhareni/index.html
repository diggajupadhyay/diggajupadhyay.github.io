Sano Okhareni

A farming village at about 1,965 m on the ridge north of Chautara, beyond the bazaar of Syaule, and the first night of the classic Panch Pokhari trek. A dirt road reaches it, so some groups start walking here. Thulo Okhareni is an hour further up.

Also written Sano Okhreni, Okhareni
