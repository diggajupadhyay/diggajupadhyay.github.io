Namrung नाम्रुङ

The entrance to upper Nubri, on a shelf above the gorge, with a checkpost and some of the better lodges on the circuit. Siringi Himal and Ganesh Himal come into view here for the first time.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
