Sankhu

A farming village in the valley west of the Namobuddha hill, on the walk down to Panauti; not the larger Sankhu north-east of Kathmandu. Below the houses the way to Panauti turns west through Sunthan, while a second path leads north toward Dhulikhel.

Also written Sankhu (Kavre), Shankhu, Sanku
