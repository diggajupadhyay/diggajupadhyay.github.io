Khumai Danda

A grassy ridge above the treeline south-east of Machhapuchhre, with a handful of basic lodges and an open view of the Annapurnas and Lamjung Himal. It is the high night of a short trek that has become popular with walkers from Pokhara.

Also written Khumai Dada, Khumai Hill
