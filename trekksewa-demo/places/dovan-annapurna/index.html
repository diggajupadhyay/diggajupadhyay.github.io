Dovan दोभान

Three or four lodges in the forest about an hour above Bamboo. Sleeping here rather than at Bamboo shortens the next day's climb to Deurali.

Also written Dobhan, Dovan (Annapurna Sanctuary)

Altitude

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
