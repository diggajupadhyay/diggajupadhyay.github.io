AnnapurnaVillage

Koto कोटो

A roadside village twenty minutes before Chame, where the Nar Khola gorge joins the Marsyangdi. The police post here checks Nar Phu restricted-area permits.

Also written Koto Qupar

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
