Jomsom जोमसोम

The windy administrative town of Mustang with an airstrip, banks and a permit checkpoint. Flights run in the early morning before the valley wind picks up.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
