Bhimtang भिमथाङ

A broad meadow of lodges beside a glacier moraine, with the first beds after the pass. Manaslu, Himlung and Cheo Himal ring the skyline. Older sources list it at 3,590 m.

Also written Bimthang, Bimtang

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
