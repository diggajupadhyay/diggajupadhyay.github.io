Kalopani कालोपानी

A lodge village in pine forest with peaks on every side: Dhaulagiri, Tukuche Peak, the Nilgiris and Annapurna I. Lete is twenty minutes further down the road.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
