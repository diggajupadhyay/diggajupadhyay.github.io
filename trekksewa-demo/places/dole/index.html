Dole दोले

The first lodges above the forest in the Gokyo valley, in a stream gully facing Kangtega and Thamserku. It is 600 m higher than Namche, and many people first feel the altitude here.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
