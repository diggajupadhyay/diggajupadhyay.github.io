Shey Gompa शे गुम्बा

A small red-walled monastery at the meeting of two rivers, below the Crystal Mountain that pilgrims circle on foot. A few houses and a campsite stand beside it. Heights from 4,160 to 4,500 m are quoted.

Also written Shey, Crystal Monastery
