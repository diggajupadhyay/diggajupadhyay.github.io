Tatopani तातोपानी

A hot spring piped into stone spouts beside the trail between Khorlabesi and Dobhan, with a couple of simple lodges. Not the larger Tatopani on the Kali Gandaki.

Also written Tatopani (Gorkha), Tatopani (Budhi Gandaki)
