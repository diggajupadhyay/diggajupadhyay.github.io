EverestVillage

Junbesi जुनबेसी

A well-kept Sherpa village around an old monastery in the upper Solu valley, with comfortable lodges, apple orchards and a school built by Hillary's Himalayan Trust. A good place to spend a rest day.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
