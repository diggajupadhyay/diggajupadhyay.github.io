AnnapurnaVillage

Ghyaru

An old stone village on a terrace about 400 m above the valley floor, reached by a long set of switchbacks. Annapurna II, III and IV line up across the valley.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
