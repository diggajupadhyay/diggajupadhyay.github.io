Ghami घमी

One of the larger villages of Lo, a block of whitewashed houses above terraced fields. Just across the river on the way to Tsarang stands the longest mani wall in Mustang.

Also written Ghemi

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
