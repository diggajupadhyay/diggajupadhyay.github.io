Chetchet

A few shops and very simple lodges on the Tamakoshi road beside a tall waterfall. The Rolwaling trail leaves the road here, crosses the river on a suspension bridge and climbs straight up to Simigaon.

Also written Chhetchhet, Chhet Chhet
