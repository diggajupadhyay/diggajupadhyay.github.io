Shaklung

A Gurung village on and just below the highest hill of the Royal Trek, with a view north to Lamjung Himal and Manaslu and east into the Madi valley. Most itineraries print 1,730 m, but maps put the hilltop at about 1,400 m.

Also written Syaklung, Syaklung Kot, Shanklung
