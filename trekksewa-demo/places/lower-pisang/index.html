Lower Pisang

The newer half of Pisang, on the valley floor beside the road. It has more lodges than the upper village and is the start of the low, easier route to Manang through Humde.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
