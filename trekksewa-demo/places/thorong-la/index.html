AnnapurnaPass

Thorong La थोरङ ला

The pass between Manang and Mustang and the high point of the Annapurna Circuit, marked by prayer flags and a small tea hut in season. Wind builds from mid-morning, so parties cross early.

Also written Thorung La, Thorong La Pass

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.

Tip. Aim to be on top by nine or ten. Turn back if cloud and snow close in before you reach the pass.
