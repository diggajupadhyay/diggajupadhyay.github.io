Chhoser छोसेर

A scatter of hamlets in the valley north-east of Lo Manthang, visited for the Jhong cave, a five-storey warren of rooms cut into a cliff, and the small Nyphu and Garphu monasteries built against the rock nearby.

Also written Chosar, Garphu
