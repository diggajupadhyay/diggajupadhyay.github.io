Larjung लार्जुङ

A village on the broad gravel bed of the Kali Gandaki below the east face of Dhaulagiri, with Kobang next door. The long side trip to the Dhaulagiri Icefall viewpoint starts here.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
