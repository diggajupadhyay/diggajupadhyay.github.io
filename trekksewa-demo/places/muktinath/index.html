Muktinath मुक्तिनाथ

A pilgrimage site sacred to Hindus and Buddhists at the foot of the Thorong La. Lodges cluster in Ranipauwa, a short walk below the temple.

Also written Ranipauwa

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
