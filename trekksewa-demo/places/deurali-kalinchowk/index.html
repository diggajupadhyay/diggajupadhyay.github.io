RolwalingVillage

Deurali देउराली

A hamlet with a few small hotels in a saddle on the Kalinchowk road, at about 2,330 m and an hour or so on foot from Charikot. The long climb to Kuri begins here, with next to no shelter on the way up.

Also written Deurali Bhanjyang, Suspa Deurali, Deurali (Charikot)
