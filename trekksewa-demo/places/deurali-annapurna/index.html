Deurali देउराली

The last lodges before the avalanche-prone stretch of the Modi Khola gorge and the usual night stop before the Sanctuary. The forest ends just below; ahead the valley is open rock and grass.

Also written Deurali (Annapurna Sanctuary)

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
