RolwalingVillage

Beding बेदिङ

The main village of the Rolwaling Sherpas: stone houses, a monastery and a school beside the river, with the south side of Gaurishankar directly above. Many Everest climbing Sherpas come from here.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
