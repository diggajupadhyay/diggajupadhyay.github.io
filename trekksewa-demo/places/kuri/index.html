RolwalingVillage

Kuri कुरी

The lodge village below the Kalinchowk shrine, at the end of the rough road from Charikot and beside the lower station of the cable car. Its height is published as anything from 3,200 to 3,450 m; the ground itself is at about 3,370 m.

Also written Kuri Village, Kuri Bazaar

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
