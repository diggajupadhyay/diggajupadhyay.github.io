Golphu Bhanjyang

A Tamang village in a notch on the ridge between Chipling and Kutumsang, with several lodges and a few shops. Many groups stop here for lunch on the long day from Chisapani.

Also written Gul Bhanjyang, Golfu Bhanjyang
