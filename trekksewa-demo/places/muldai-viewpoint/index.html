Muldai Viewpoint

A bare hilltop about half an hour above Dobato, higher than Poon Hill and far quieter. The view takes in Dhaulagiri, Annapurna South, Hiunchuli and Machhapuchhre.

Also written Mulde Peak, Muldai Peak

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
