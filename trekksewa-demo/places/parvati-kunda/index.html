Parvati Kunda पार्वती कुण्ड

A small sacred lake in a hollow of pasture and pine forest at about 2,600 m, an hour's climb above Gatlang. It is holy to Hindu and Buddhist pilgrims alike, and the trail to Somdang passes its shore.

Also written Parbati Kunda, Parvati Kund
