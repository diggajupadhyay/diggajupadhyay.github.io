Tharepati थाडेपाटी

A windy ridge-top group of lodges where the trail from Gosaikunda meets the Helambu circuit, quoted between 3,640 and 3,690 m. It is the highest night on the Helambu trek, with a dawn view east to Dorje Lakpa and the Jugal peaks.

Also written Thadepati, Tharepati Pass

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
