AnnapurnaVillage

Timang

A small village on a shelf above the Marsyangdi gorge with a wide view back to Manaslu. It is a common lunch stop between Dharapani and Chame.

Altitude
2,750 m9,022 ft
Altitude zone
High altitude

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
