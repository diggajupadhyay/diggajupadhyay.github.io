Chele चैले

A small village on a ledge above the point where the Kali Gandaki flows out from under a huge block of red rock. The steep climb from the bridge marks the change from the valley floor to the high, dry ridge country beyond.

Also written Chaile, Tsaile

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
