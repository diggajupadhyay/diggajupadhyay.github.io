AnnapurnaVillage

Braga

A village half an hour before Manang, built in tiers below a monastery that is among the oldest in the district. The trail to Ice Lake starts here.

Also written Bhraka, Braka

Altitude
3,450 m11,319 ft
Altitude zone
High altitude

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
