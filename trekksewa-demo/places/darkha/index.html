Darkha

A hillside village in the Ankhu Khola valley, well south of Borang, and the last night on the longer walk out of Ruby Valley. Jeeps for Dhading Besi leave from Darkha Phedi in the valley below.

Also written Darkha Gaun, Darkha Gaon
