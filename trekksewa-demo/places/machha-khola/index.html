Machha Khola माछाखोला

The usual start of the Manaslu and Tsum treks: a line of lodges above the Budhi Gandaki where the bus and jeep road from Kathmandu ends for most travellers. The name means fish stream.

Also written Machhakhola, Machha Khola Gaon
