Khopra Danda खोप्रा डाँडा

A grassy spur high above the Kali Gandaki with a community lodge on its crest. Dhaulagiri stands directly across the gorge and Annapurna South rises close behind the lodge.

Also written Khopra Ridge, Kopra Ridge

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
