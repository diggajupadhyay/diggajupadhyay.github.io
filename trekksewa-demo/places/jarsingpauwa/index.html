Jarsingpauwa

A roadside bazaar at about 1,800 m on the ridge north-east of Sankhu, where the track from Chisapani meets the road between Sankhu and Melamchi. Local buses and jeeps run down toward Kathmandu, so it is the bail-out point of a long day.
