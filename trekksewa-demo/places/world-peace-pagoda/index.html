World Peace Pagoda विश्व शान्ति स्तूप

A white Buddhist stupa on the forested ridge south of Phewa Lake, looking across Pokhara to the Annapurna range. You reach it by boat and a climb of stone steps, or by taxi to a car park a short walk below.

Also written Shanti Stupa, Peace Pagoda, Peace Stupa
