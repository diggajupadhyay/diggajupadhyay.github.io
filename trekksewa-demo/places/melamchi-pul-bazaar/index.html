Melamchi Pul Bazaar मेलम्ची पुल बजार

A hot, low market town at the meeting of the Melamchi and Indrawati rivers, and the traditional end of the Helambu trek. Debris floods in June 2021 destroyed the riverside part of the bazaar. Buses run from here to Kathmandu.
