RolwalingTown

Dolakha Bazaar दोलखा बजार

An old Newar trading town a few kilometres beyond Charikot, known for the open-roofed shrine of Dolakha Bhimsen. The road to Singati and the Rolwaling trailhead passes through on its way down to the Tamakoshi.
