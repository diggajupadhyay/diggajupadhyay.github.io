Tikhedhunga तिखेढुङ्गा

A string of lodges beside the Bhurungdi Khola at the foot of the Ulleri staircase. It is the usual first night on the walk to Ghorepani, and jeeps from Pokhara come this far, with Hile just below.

Also written Tikhedhungga, Tirkhedhunga
