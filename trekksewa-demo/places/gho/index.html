Gho

A cluster of lodges where the forest of the Dudh Khola valley gives way to farmland, about four hours below Bhimtang. It lies in Manang district, inside the Annapurna Conservation Area.

Also written Goa, Gowa

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
