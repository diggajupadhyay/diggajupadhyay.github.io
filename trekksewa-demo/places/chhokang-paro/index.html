Chhokang Paro छेकम्पार

Twin villages, Chhokang and Paro, on a flat shelf at the entrance to upper Tsum. The valley opens out here after the steep climb from the gorge, with Ganesh Himal to the south and Himalchuli behind you.

Also written Chhekampar, Chhokangparo, Chekampar
