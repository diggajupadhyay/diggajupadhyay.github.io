EverestVillage

Chheplung छेप्लुङ

A village half an hour north of Lukla where the old trail from Jiri and the Solu road-heads meets the main Everest trail. Walkers coming up from Surke join here and never need to enter Lukla.

Also written Cheplung

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
