Ama Dablam Base Camp आमा दब्लम आधार शिविर

A broad grassy flat beneath the west face of Ama Dablam, about three hours' walk from Pangboche. In October and November it fills with expedition tents; for the rest of the year it is empty yak pasture.

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
