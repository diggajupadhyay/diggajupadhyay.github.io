ManasluLake

Birendra Tal वीरेन्द्र ताल

A milky-blue lake at the foot of the Manaslu glacier, under an hour's walk from Sama Gaun. Blocks of ice fall into the water in the warmer months. Usually given as 3,691 m, though some maps show it lower.
