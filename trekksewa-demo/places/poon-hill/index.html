Poon Hill पुनहिल

A grassy summit with a viewing tower, 45 minutes to an hour above Ghorepani. At sunrise the skyline runs from Dhaulagiri across the Kali Gandaki gorge to Annapurna South and Machhapuchhre.

Also written Pun Hill
