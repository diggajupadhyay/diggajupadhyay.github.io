Rechi

A small riverside settlement with a lodge or two in the middle of the gorge, at about 2,940 m. Useful for breaking the long stage between Chhepka and Chunuwar.

High altitude. Altitude illness is possible, especially after a fast ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
