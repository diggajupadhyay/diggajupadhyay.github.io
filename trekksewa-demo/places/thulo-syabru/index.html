LangtangVillage

Thulo Syabru ठूलो स्याफ्रु

A Tamang village strung down a steep spur between the Langtang Khola and the Gosaikunda ridge, and the link between the two treks. With the Syabrubesi road cut by the August 2026 flood, Langtang valley treks have been starting from here.
