Timure टिमुरे

A roadside village on the Bhote Koshi a few kilometres short of the Rasuwagadhi border fort, once an optional night on the Tamang Heritage Trail. Reports say the flood of 26 August 2026 destroyed most of it; treat it as closed until told otherwise.
