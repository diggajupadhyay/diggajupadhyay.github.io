French Pass फ्रेन्च पास

The 5,360 m saddle between the Chhonbardan Glacier and Hidden Valley and the highest point of the circuit. It is named after the 1950 French expedition, which reconnoitred Dhaulagiri here before turning to Annapurna.

Also written French Col
