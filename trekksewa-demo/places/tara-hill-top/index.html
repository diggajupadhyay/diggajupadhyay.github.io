Tara Hill Top

A clearing on the forested ridge west of Sikles with a dawn view of Annapurna II, Lamjung Himal and Machhapuchhre. Sources give its height as 2,800 to 3,056 m. Shelter is one very basic teahouse, or your own tent.

Also written Tara Top, Tara Hilltop
