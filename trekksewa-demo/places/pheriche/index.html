EverestVillage

Pheriche फेरिचे

A windy valley-floor village with the Himalayan Rescue Association aid post, staffed by volunteer doctors in the main seasons. Most groups pass through on the way down.

Altitude
4,240 m13,911 ft
Altitude zone

Very high altitude. Altitude illness is common without gradual ascent. Check your symptoms if you have a headache here.
