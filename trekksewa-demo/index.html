How it works

Three steps from "I would like to" to "I am going"

  1. 1Answer three questions

    How many days, how much walking, and which month. That is all we need to suggest treks that suit you.

  2. 2Get your plan

    Each day set out in plain words: where you walk, how long it takes, where you sleep, and what it is likely to cost.

  3. 3Go with good people

    Send the plan to arrange a guide, a porter or a whole trip. Print it, save it, or share it with your family.
