What a rescue costs

About USD 3,000–6,000 from the upper Khumbu, USD 2,500–4,000 from Annapurna Base Camp to Pokhara and USD 2,500–5,500 from Langtang to Kathmandu; insure for helicopter evacuation up to 6,000 m

These are operator and agency estimates for 2025–26 and differ about twofold for the same pick-up, with some quotes for Gorak Shep or Everest Base Camp reaching USD 8,000–12,000; the UK government puts the price at GBP 1,000–2,000 or more per flying hour. Check that your policy names helicopter evacuation and covers trekking to your highest point (5,545 m on Kala Patthar, 5,416 m on the Thorong La), because many standard and credit-card policies stop at 3,000 m or lower.

We do not sell or recommend insurance, and this list is not advice on any particular policy. Read the wording, and ask the insurer in writing if anything is unclear.
