Privacy

The short version: there are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and what you save stays on your device.

Last updated November 2026.

What stays on your device

Saved treks, trek dates, packing lists, budget figures, altitude-check history, your emergency card, a loaded GPX track and any track you record are stored in your browser's own storage. They are never sent to TrekkSewa. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

Location

The emergency page and the GPX viewer ask for your position only when you press a button to use it. Your position is shown to you and placed in a message that you choose to send from your own phone. We do not receive or store it.

Requests your browser makes to others

  • Weather. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo. Your browser sends the coordinates of the place you are looking at, not your own position, along with your IP address as with any web request.
  • Maps. Map images come from OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap tile servers, which see your IP address and which map areas you view.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

We use no analytics, no advertising networks, no social-media trackers and no cookies of our own.

Partner applications

If you apply to be listed as a guide, porter, agency or shop, the details you submit are used only to review your application, contact you and, with your approval, publish your profile. We do not sell or share applicant details. You can ask for your application or profile to be corrected or deleted at any time.

Until online applications are switched on, the application form keeps your answers on your device only.

Children

The site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If the way we handle information changes, for example when partner accounts are introduced, this page will be updated before the change takes effect.
