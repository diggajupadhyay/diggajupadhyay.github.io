Dolpo, Rara & the Far West पश्चिम नेपाल

Turquoise lakes, remote passes and the least-visited trails in the country.

Treks
4
Places
47
Best months
Mar–Nov
Highest place listed
5,350 mKang La (Dolpo)

The Karnali and far-western hills hold Nepal's largest lake, Rara, and its deepest, Phoksundo. Beyond Phoksundo lies Upper Dolpo, a high, dry plateau of Tibetan culture that needs an expensive restricted-area permit and a camping crew.

Getting here takes two flights or days of driving, services are sparse and the weather closes airstrips without notice. In return the trails are almost empty.

When to go

April to early June and September to early November. Upper Dolpo is in the rain shadow and is at its best from June to September, though flights in and out are less reliable then.

Getting there

Fly Kathmandu to Nepalgunj, then on to Juphal for Dolpo or Talcha for Rara. Jumla and Khaptad can also be reached by long road journeys.

Permits used in Western Nepal

All permits

Khaptad National Park

National park

Khaptad National Park entry permit · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,500person, single entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person

Where: At the park entry post on the way in, or in advance at the national parks counter in the Tourist Service Centre, Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu.

NPR 3,000 for foreigners, NPR 1,500 for SAARC nationals and NPR 100 for Nepali citizens are the figures in the Nepal Tourism Board's current fee table, which it says follows the parks department; children under 10 are free. Older pages still show less (NPR 1,000 or NPR 1,500 for foreigners), and some agencies repeat them. Budget for the higher, current figure, carry a margin for tax, and ask at the park office if you are charged differently.

Shey Phoksundo National Park

National park

Shey Phoksundo National Park entry permit · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,500person, single entry

Where: Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, or the park entry post on the trail in from Juphal.

The Nepal Tourism Board page gives NPR 3,000 and NPR 1,500 per entry; whether VAT is added on top is unconfirmed, as for the other national parks. Park headquarters are at Sumduwa. The Dolpo restricted-area permits are separate.

Lower Dolpo RAP

Restricted area

Lower Dolpo restricted area permit · Department of Immigration

All foreign nationalsUSD 20person per week, then USD 5 a day

Where: Department of Immigration in Kathmandu, through a registered trekking agency only.

Since 22 March 2026 the permit can be issued to a single trekker; before that the minimum was two. You still have to book through a registered agency and walk with a licensed guide, with at most seven trekkers per guide. The Shey Phoksundo park permit is extra.

Agency required

Rara National Park

National park

Rara National Park entry permit · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,500person, single entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person

Where: At the park entry posts on the way to the lake, or in advance at the Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu.

All three rates match the Nepal Tourism Board's current fee table; children under 10 are free. Whether VAT is added to the foreigner fee is unconfirmed. Rara Lake itself needs no restricted-area permit.

TIMS card

Registration

Trekkers' Information Management System card · Nepal Tourism Board and the Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal

Foreign nationalsNPR 2,000person, per trek
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, per trek

Where: Only through a registered trekking agency, which applies in the Nepal Tourism Board's online system with your route and your guide's details. Individual trekkers can no longer buy one over the counter.

Since the revised rules of 31 March 2023 the card is tied to trekking with a licensed guide on the tourism board's listed routes. It is not used in Khumbu, where the municipality's Trek Card took its place in 2017. For restricted areas the published rules pull both ways: TAAN's pages say TIMS applies in every trekking area, restricted ones included, yet also list visitors holding Department of Immigration permits among those who do not need one, and agencies disagree. Your agency, which is compulsory there anyway, will know what the checkposts on your route ask for. Reports from 2025–26 describe patchy checks of the card, especially in Annapurna.

Agency required

Upper Dolpo RAP

Restricted area

Upper Dolpo restricted area permit · Department of Immigration

All foreign nationalsUSD 50person per day

Where: Department of Immigration in Kathmandu, through a registered trekking agency only.

The Nepal Tourism Board's permit fee table now lists Upper Dolpo at USD 50 a person a day, the same as Upper Mustang, in place of the old USD 500 for the first ten days. For a stay of ten days the cost is unchanged; a shorter crossing costs less and a longer one costs USD 50 for every extra day. Since 22 March 2026 the permit can be issued to a single trekker, but you still need a registered agency and a licensed guide (at most seven trekkers per guide). The Shey Phoksundo park permit and the Lower Dolpo permit for the approach are extra. Ask your agency to count the days you will spend inside the restricted area.

Agency required
