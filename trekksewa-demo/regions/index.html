Trekking regions of Nepal

Where you go decides how you get there, how high you sleep, which permits you need and how many people you share the trail with.

Everest & Solukhumbu सगरमाथा

Sherpa villages, big glaciers and the highest trails in Nepal.

The Everest region is the Khumbu and Solu country of north-eastern Nepal: a staircase of Sherpa villages climbing from terraced farms to the moraines below Everest, Lhotse, Cho Oyu and Ama Dablam. Trails are wide, well marked and lined with lodges, which makes the logistics easy even though the altitude is not.

Annapurna अन्नपूर्ण

From rice terraces to a 5,416 m pass, with a road never far away.

The Annapurna massif sits just north of Pokhara, and its trails cover the widest range of landscapes of any Nepali region: subtropical villages, rhododendron forest, alpine sanctuaries and, on the northern side, the dry valleys of Manang and the Kali Gandaki.

Mustang मुस्ताङ

High desert, wind-carved cliffs and walled towns behind the Himalaya.

Mustang follows the Kali Gandaki north from Jomsom to the Tibetan border. Lower Mustang, around Kagbeni, Marpha and Muktinath, is open to anyone with an Annapurna permit. Upper Mustang, the former kingdom of Lo, is a restricted area that needs a special permit and a registered agency.

Langtang & Helambu लाङटाङ

The closest high mountains to Kathmandu, reached by road.

Langtang National Park starts a day's drive north of Kathmandu. The main valley climbs through forest to Kyanjin Gompa beneath Langtang Lirung, with Tamang and Tibetan-influenced villages along the way. To the south the sacred lakes of Gosaikunda and the ridges of Helambu link back toward the Kathmandu Valley on foot.

Manaslu & Tsum मनास्लु

A full Himalayan circuit with far fewer people.

The Manaslu circuit follows the Budhi Gandaki gorge from the mid-hills to the Tibetan-influenced villages of Nubri, crosses the Larkya La at 5,106 m and drops to the Annapurna side. The side valley of Tsum, opened to trekkers in 2008, keeps a strong Buddhist culture.

Kanchenjunga & Makalu पूर्वी नेपाल

Long, quiet approaches to the third and fifth highest mountains.

East of Everest the trails become long and lightly travelled. Kanchenjunga, on the border with Sikkim, takes three weeks to visit both base camps. Makalu Base Camp climbs from the hot Arun valley, among the lowest in Nepal, to the foot of an 8,485 m peak.

Dolpo, Rara & the Far West पश्चिम नेपाल

Turquoise lakes, remote passes and the least-visited trails in the country.

The Karnali and far-western hills hold Nepal's largest lake, Rara, and its deepest, Phoksundo. Beyond Phoksundo lies Upper Dolpo, a high, dry plateau of Tibetan culture that needs an expensive restricted-area permit and a camping crew.

Rolwaling & Gaurishankar रोल्वालिङ

A narrow Sherpa valley and a glacial lake below Gaurishankar.

Rolwaling is a steep, sparsely populated valley in Dolakha district, between Langtang and Everest. The trail climbs to the summer village of Na and the moraine-dammed lake of Tsho Rolpa under the twin summits of Gaurishankar.

Kathmandu Valley Rim काठमाडौं उपत्यका

Forest ridges and Himalayan sunrises a bus ride from the city.

The hills that ring the Kathmandu Valley rise to about 2,700 m and carry a network of forest trails, village paths and viewpoints. On a clear morning the skyline runs from Annapurna to Everest.
