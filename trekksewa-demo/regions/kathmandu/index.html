Kathmandu Valley Rim काठमाडौं उपत्यका

Forest ridges and Himalayan sunrises a bus ride from the city.

Treks
1
Places
18
Best months
Oct–Mar
Highest place listed
2,782 mPhulchoki

The hills that ring the Kathmandu Valley rise to about 2,700 m and carry a network of forest trails, village paths and viewpoints. On a clear morning the skyline runs from Annapurna to Everest.

These are low-altitude walks of one to three days with lodges or hotels each night. They work as a warm-up before a bigger trek, as a first hike with children, or when you only have a weekend.

When to go

October to April for mountain views. It is walkable all year, but expect haze in late spring and leeches and cloud in the monsoon.

Getting there

Local buses and taxis reach Sundarijal, Nagarkot, Dhulikhel and the other trailheads in an hour or two.

Shivapuri National Park

National park

Shivapuri Nagarjun National Park entry ticket · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 1,000person, per day and entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 600person, per day and entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person, per day and entry

Where: At the park gate, for example at Sundarijal.

The park's own fee table charges per day and says SAARC and other foreign visitors must go in with a nature guide. We could not confirm how that is applied to trekkers walking through from Sundarijal to Chisapani. Bicycles and vehicles pay extra.
