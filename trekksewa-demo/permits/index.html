Trekking permits and fees

Every trek in Nepal needs at least one piece of paper. Here is what each permit is, what it costs, and where you actually get it.

Check before you pay. Fees and rules change, sometimes mid-season. Treat these as a planning guide and confirm with the Nepal Tourism Board, the Department of Immigration or a registered agency before you travel. Last reviewed 2026-11.

Which permits does my trek need?

Do I have to hire a guide?

Required on paper since 1 April 2023; not applied in Khumbu, and unevenly enforced in Annapurna and Langtang

The Nepal Tourism Board's revised TIMS provision, effective 31 March 2023, says foreign trekkers on its listed national-park and conservation-area routes (Everest, Annapurna, Langtang and Manaslu among them) must walk with a licensed guide and carry a TIMS card issued by a registered agency. Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality rejected the rule and still registers independent trekkers at Lukla; in Annapurna and Langtang the rule stands, but 2025–26 reports conflict, with agencies saying guide licences are checked and independent accounts saying TIMS checkposts are mostly unstaffed, while restricted areas always require a guide and an agency.

Registration

The trekker registration card used outside Khumbu and the restricted areas.

TIMS card

Registration

Trekkers' Information Management System card · Nepal Tourism Board and the Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal

Foreign nationalsNPR 2,000person, per trek
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, per trek

Where: Only through a registered trekking agency, which applies in the Nepal Tourism Board's online system with your route and your guide's details. Individual trekkers can no longer buy one over the counter.

Since the revised rules of 31 March 2023 the card is tied to trekking with a licensed guide on the tourism board's listed routes. It is not used in Khumbu, where the municipality's Trek Card took its place in 2017. For restricted areas the published rules pull both ways: TAAN's pages say TIMS applies in every trekking area, restricted ones included, yet also list visitors holding Department of Immigration permits among those who do not need one, and agencies disagree. Your agency, which is compulsory there anyway, will know what the checkposts on your route ask for. Reports from 2025–26 describe patchy checks of the card, especially in Annapurna.

Agency required

National parks

Entry permits issued by the Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation.

Sagarmatha National Park

National park

Sagarmatha National Park entry permit · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,500person, single entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person

Where: At the park gate in Monjo, or in advance at the park counter in the Tourist Service Centre, Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu.

Children under 10 enter free. Nepali press reports from 2024 to 2026 give the same NPR 3,000, 1,500 and 100. Whether 13% VAT is added for foreigners is disputed: an independent guide says tax has been folded into park fees since 2019, while several agency sites still quote NPR 3,390. We found no official statement, so carry the difference in cash. Keep the permit handy; it is checked at the gate.

Langtang National Park

National park

Langtang National Park entry permit · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,500person, single entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person

Where: At the park checkpoint in Dhunche on the drive to Syabrubesi, or in advance at the Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu.

The Nepal Tourism Board and current guides agree on NPR 3,000 and NPR 1,500. Some agencies add 13% VAT to the foreigner fee (NPR 3,390); we could not confirm that it is charged, so carry the difference. NPR 100 for Nepali citizens is the standard mountain-park rate; the tourism board's page still shows an older figure.

Shivapuri National Park

National park

Shivapuri Nagarjun National Park entry ticket · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 1,000person, per day and entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 600person, per day and entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person, per day and entry

Where: At the park gate, for example at Sundarijal.

The park's own fee table charges per day and says SAARC and other foreign visitors must go in with a nature guide. We could not confirm how that is applied to trekkers walking through from Sundarijal to Chisapani. Bicycles and vehicles pay extra.

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,500person, single entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person

Where: Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, or the park entry posts at Num and Seduwa on the trail.

Khaptad National Park

National park

Khaptad National Park entry permit · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,500person, single entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person

Where: At the park entry post on the way in, or in advance at the national parks counter in the Tourist Service Centre, Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu.

Conservation areas

Entry permits for areas managed by conservation trusts and local councils.

ACAP

Conservation area

Annapurna Conservation Area Permit · National Trust for Nature Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, single entry

Where: Online at NTNC's e-permit site, or in person at the Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, and the Nepal Tourism Board office in Pokhara. A permit issued at a trail checkpost costs double.

The fee includes tax and children under 10 do not need a permit. Paying online by card adds a 2.9% gateway charge. Covers every trek in the Annapurna range and Lower Mustang; Upper Mustang and Nar Phu need a restricted-area permit as well.

Checked against: NTNC online entry permit portal (fees, tax, checkpost surcharge), NTNC: Annapurna Conservation Area Project

MCAP

Conservation area

Manaslu Conservation Area Permit · National Trust for Nature Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, single entry

Where: Online at NTNC's e-permit site or at the Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu. In practice your agency arranges it with the restricted-area permit.

The fee includes tax and children under 10 do not need a permit. The Manaslu circuit finishes inside the Annapurna Conservation Area, so you need an ACAP as well.

Checked against: NTNC online entry permit portal (ACAP, MCAP and GCAP fees)

Gaurishankar CA

Conservation area

Gaurishankar Conservation Area Permit · National Trust for Nature Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 1,000person, single entry

Where: Online at NTNC's e-permit site or at the Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu. A permit issued at a trail checkpost costs double.

The fee includes tax and children under 10 do not need a permit. NTNC lists Rolwaling and Tsho Rolpa, Kalinchowk and the Shivalaya walk-in towards Everest under this permit. Upper Rolwaling needs the Dolakha restricted-area permit too.

Checked against: NTNC online entry permit portal (ACAP, MCAP and GCAP fees), NTNC: Gaurishankar Conservation Area Project

Kanchenjunga CA

Conservation area

Kanchenjunga Conservation Area Permit · Kanchenjunga Conservation Area Management Council

Foreign nationalsNPR 2,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 500person, single entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person

Where: Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, normally arranged by your agency with the restricted-area permit. Foreign trekkers are also registered at check posts on the trail, for example at Ghunsa.

NPR 2,000 for foreigners, NPR 500 for SAARC nationals and NPR 100 for Nepali citizens are the figures in the Nepal Tourism Board's current fee table; children under 10 are free. A few agency pages list NPR 3,000, which is the NTNC rate and does not apply here, because this area is run by a local council under the Department of National Parks.

Checked against: Nepal Tourism Board: Park Entry Fees (fee table, read 22 November 2026), Nepal Tourism Board: Kanchenjunga Conservation Area, Xtrekka: Nepal trekking permits 2026, costs and rules by trek

Api Nampa CA

Conservation area

Api Nampa Conservation Area Permit · Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation

Foreign nationalsNPR 2,000person, single entry
SAARC nationalsNPR 500person, single entry
Nepali citizensNPR 100person

Where: At the conservation area office in Darchula, or in advance at the Tourist Service Centre in Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, where your agency can arrange it.

The Nepal Tourism Board's current park fee table, which it says follows the parks department, lists NPR 2,000 for foreigners, NPR 500 for SAARC nationals and NPR 100 for Nepali citizens, with children under 10 free. Most 2025–26 agency lists give the same foreigner and SAARC rates. Older pages that show NPR 1,000 or NPR 3,000 plus VAT do not match it. The department's own website carried no fee table when we checked on 22 November 2026, and whether VAT is added is unconfirmed, so carry a small margin. The Byas valley towards the border needs the Darchula restricted-area permit as well.

Local permits

Entry fees charged by rural municipalities.

Khumbu municipality permit

Local permit

Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality entry permit · Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality

Foreign nationalsNPR 3,000person
SAARC and Chinese nationalsNPR 2,000person

Where: At the municipality's tourist information centre in Lukla when you land, or at the Monjo checkpoint if you walk in from the road. It cannot be bought in Kathmandu.

The municipality calls it a Trek Card: staff take your passport details and a photo, which takes about 15 minutes. It has stood in for the TIMS card in Khumbu since October 2017, when the fee was NPR 2,000. The municipality's own page now lists NPR 3,000, with NPR 2,000 for SAARC and Chinese passport holders, and its 2024–25 visitor and revenue figures fit that split. A few agency pages say every foreigner pays NPR 3,000. Bring rupees in cash.

Checked against: Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality: Trek-Card (official fee and where it is issued), Kantipur: Khumbu Pasanglhamu Rural Municipality collected 150 million in tourism fees, May 2025, The Kathmandu Post: Rural municipality starts collecting tourism fee in Everest region, October 2017

Restricted areas

Special permits issued only through a registered trekking agency, for groups of at least two with a licensed guide.

Manaslu RAP

Restricted area

Manaslu restricted area permit · Department of Immigration

September to NovemberUSD 100person for the first 7 days, then USD 15 a day
December to AugustUSD 75person for the first 7 days, then USD 10 a day

Where: Department of Immigration in Kathmandu (the Pokhara immigration office also issues them), through a registered trekking agency only.

Since 22 March 2026 the permit can be issued to a single trekker; before that the minimum was two. You still have to book through a registered agency and walk with a licensed guide, with at most seven trekkers per guide. The restricted area is Chumnubri Rural Municipality: it starts at Jagat and continues over the Larkya La. You need the MCAP and the ACAP as well.

Agency required

Checked against: Department of Immigration: Trekking Route and Permit Fee, The Kathmandu Post: Immigration eases rules for solo trekkers in restricted areas, 22 March 2026, Department of Immigration: Notice regarding trekking permits for restricted areas, 23 March 2026

Upper Mustang RAP

Restricted area

Upper Mustang restricted area permit · Department of Immigration

All foreign nationalsUSD 50person per day

Where: Department of Immigration in Kathmandu (the Pokhara immigration office also issues them), through a registered trekking agency only.

The old flat fee of USD 500 for ten days was scrapped by a cabinet decision in November 2025. The Department of Immigration's fee table now shows USD 50 a day with no minimum stay, so a ten-day trip costs the same and a shorter one costs less. Since 22 March 2026 the permit can be issued to a single trekker, but you still need a registered agency and a licensed guide (at most seven trekkers per guide). The permit is checked at Kagbeni, and you need an ACAP as well.

Agency required

Checked against: Department of Immigration: Trekking Route and Permit Fee, The Kathmandu Post: Nepal scraps $500 upper Mustang trekking fee for foreigners, sets $50 daily, 20 November 2025, The Rising Nepal: Foreigners allowed for solo trekking in Upper Mustang

Upper Dolpo RAP

Restricted area

Upper Dolpo restricted area permit · Department of Immigration

All foreign nationalsUSD 50person per day

Where: Department of Immigration in Kathmandu, through a registered trekking agency only.

The Nepal Tourism Board's permit fee table now lists Upper Dolpo at USD 50 a person a day, the same as Upper Mustang, in place of the old USD 500 for the first ten days. For a stay of ten days the cost is unchanged; a shorter crossing costs less and a longer one costs USD 50 for every extra day. Since 22 March 2026 the permit can be issued to a single trekker, but you still need a registered agency and a licensed guide (at most seven trekkers per guide). The Shey Phoksundo park permit and the Lower Dolpo permit for the approach are extra. Ask your agency to count the days you will spend inside the restricted area.
