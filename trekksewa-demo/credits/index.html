Data and credits

Where the information comes from, the open projects the site is built on, and the medical references behind the safety tools.

Our own content

Route guides, place descriptions, hazard notes and articles are written for TrekkSewa. Altitudes, stage plans and coordinates are compiled from general knowledge of the routes and cross-checked against published sources where we could.

Place positions

The gazetteer holds 451 places. Each started as our own estimate, which we then tested two ways: against OpenStreetMap, by looking the place up by name in the area around the estimate, and against a terrain elevation model (Copernicus DEM, through the Open-Meteo elevation service), where the ground height at the marker has to agree with the altitude we state.

  • 394 take their position from OpenStreetMap. A match had to carry the right name and be the right kind of feature, and its height had to agree with ours wherever a height was mapped.
  • 39 keep our own estimate. OpenStreetMap gave no clear match, but the estimate sits on ground at the right height. These are labelled approximate.
  • 18 we could not confirm either way. They are listed with their altitude but are not drawn on any map, because a marker in the wrong place is worse than none.

Positions are a planning aid and not survey data. Coordinates taken from OpenStreetMap are © OpenStreetMap contributors and remain under the Open Database Licence.

Trails and places to stay

The footpath drawn on each trek map, the route file you can download, and the lists of lodges, health posts, cash machines and water points all come from OpenStreetMap, read from the Protomaps daily build of 21 November 2026. 602 of the walking sections in our itineraries follow a mapped path. The remaining 33 that we can place are drawn as dashes that simply join two stops, and a section is left off the map altogether where we could not confirm where a stop is. Lodges are listed for 320 places, 2,155 in all.

This data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and is available under the Open Database Licence. It is made by volunteers: a lodge may have closed, a new one may be missing, and a path may have moved since it was mapped. If you spot a mistake, the best fix is to correct it in OpenStreetMap, where everyone benefits.

The counts of outdoor shops, cash machines and pharmacies on the gear page are counts of what was mapped around Thamel, Lakeside and Namche Bazaar on 21 November 2026. A shop counts as an outdoor shop when it is mapped as one, or when it is mapped as a clothes, sports, shoe or bag shop with a name that says trekking or mountain gear.

Prices

The package price ranges and transport fares used by the planner are list prices that trekking companies, booking sites and operators showed online, gathered on 22 November 2026. Nothing was confirmed with a company, airline or ticket counter. They are there to help you budget and to recognise a fair offer, not as a quote.
