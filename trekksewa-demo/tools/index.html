Planning tools

Small, free tools for the decisions that shape a trek. They run in your browser, keep your data on your device and work offline once opened.

Compare treks

Two or three routes side by side: length, altitude, difficulty, season and permits.

Plan my trek

Step by step: pick a trek, set your dates, see where you sleep each night, what it costs and who can help.

Packing list

A checklist built for your trek, month and altitude, with what to rent in Nepal marked.

Budget estimator

Permits, guide, porter, food, lodging and transport added up, with every assumption editable.

GPX viewer

Load a track to see its distance, climb and profile, follow it on a map and get off-track alerts.

Acclimatization checker

Test an itinerary against altitude guidelines and see where to add a night.

Altitude sickness check

Score your symptoms and get a clear next step.

Permit finder

Which permits a trek needs, what they cost and where to get them.

Insurance check

What your policy has to cover for the trek you have chosen.

Emergency & SOS

Rescue numbers, location sharing, whistle and light signal.
