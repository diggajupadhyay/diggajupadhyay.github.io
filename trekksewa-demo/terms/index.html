Terms of use

Plain terms for a planning tool. Please read the safety section even if you skip the rest.

Last updated November 2026.

Safety and your own judgement

Trekking at altitude is dangerous. TrekkSewa provides general information to help you plan. It is not professional guiding, medical advice, a weather warning service or a rescue service, and nothing on it should be relied on as your only source for a decision that affects your safety.

Trails, bridges, lodges, roads, flights, permits, fees and regulations change, sometimes overnight. Forecasts are model output and can be wrong. Map markers and lines are approximate. You are responsible for checking current conditions locally, for your choice of route, companions and equipment, and for turning back when that is the right call.

The altitude tools are educational. They do not diagnose or treat any condition. If you or a companion are unwell at altitude, descend and seek medical help.

Accuracy

We work to keep the content correct and say where we are unsure, but we give no guarantee that any information is accurate, complete or current.

Services and partners

When the partner directory opens, guides, porters, agencies and shops listed on TrekkSewa will be independent of us. Any agreement you make is between you and them. We check the credentials partners give us, but a listing is not a guarantee of their conduct or of the outcome of your trek.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the site: no automated bulk copying of the content, no false partner applications, and no attempts to interfere with how it works.

Content and licence

The text, design and software of TrekkSewa belong to TrekkSewa. You may use the site for your own trips and share links to it. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and is used under its own licence; see Data and credits.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, TrekkSewa is provided as it is, and we are not liable for loss, injury or damage arising from use of the site or reliance on its content.

Changes

These terms will change as the service grows, in particular when bookings or partner accounts are introduced. The date above shows the latest version.
